## PayMongo model (AUTHORITATIVE — apply this wording consistently)
- **Provider:** PayMongo — BSP-regulated Philippine EMI. Rails: **QRPh, GCash, Maya, cards**.
- **Why (not Stripe):** Stripe has no local PH acquiring and no QRPh (invite-only preview; workaround needs a US entity). PayMongo is PH-native with marketplace payouts.
- **Marketplace payout (hold-until-session):** collect the full amount to the **platform wallet** → **HOLD** → after the session, push an **on-demand `inhouse` wallet-to-wallet transfer** (`POST /v2/batch_transfers`, `provider:"paymongo"`) of **(booking − commission)** to the host's Linked-Account wallet. **DO NOT use PayMongo "payment splitting"** (it pays the host at settlement — violates hold-until-session). This is the equivalent of Stripe separate-charges-and-transfers.
- **Host onboarding:** PayMongo **Platforms / Linked Accounts** (hosted-redirect KYC; beta / sales-gated).
- **Bookability gate (= Stripe `payouts_enabled`):** host **`merchant.activated`** webhook + **`activation_status: activated`** + wallet **`status: activated`**.
- **No official SDK:** thin REST wrapper or community TS lib. Webhooks verified via **`Paymongo-Signature`** HMAC-SHA256 over the raw body; **`Idempotency-Key`** on POSTs.
- **Request-to-book capture (reference only — Phase 6, not edited here):** card manual-capture is a GATED advanced feature; **QRPh/GCash/Maya = capture-now → refund-on-decline**.
- Preserve the CORRECTNESS intent everywhere: hold funds until the session; webhook as source of truth; **never pay the host at booking time**.
