# FitOut

## What This Is

FitOut is an Airbnb-style two-sided marketplace for **fitness and recreational spaces** — gyms, courts (pickleball, tennis, basketball), studios (yoga, dance), and private/home gym setups — that people can book flexibly by the hour or by the day. Hosts list their space and availability; people search, see real availability, and book a time slot. A signature feature is **group bookings**: an organizer reserves a space and invites friends, confirming how many attendees are coming.

The product is optimized first for the **person booking** (the demand side is the center of gravity), with hosts as the supply that makes booking possible.

**As shipped in v1.0**, a listing carries one of two host-set **occupancy modes**, and this turned out to be the shape of the product rather than a detail:

- **Exclusive** (the default) — one booker takes the space for a time window. Overlaps are made impossible by a Postgres GiST `EXCLUDE` constraint.
- **Open capacity / drop-in** — many independent bookers share one day up to an admissions cap, each paying per head (a drop-in gym, a host-run open court). Here the guarantee is a per-`(listing, date)` admissions counter claimed under an advisory lock, not the exclusion constraint (D-123).

Group bookings sit on top of a *paid* exclusive booking: the organizer pays, then invites by link or email, and account-less attendees RSVP against a transactionally-frozen capacity cap.

## Core Value

**Find & book a space.** If everything else fails, a person must be able to search for a fitness space, see real availability, and reserve a time slot — smoothly and with confidence the booking is real.

## Current Milestone: v1.1 Front-End Polish & Placeholder Design System

**Goal:** Give every surface v1.0 shipped a coherent, finished-looking UI built on a swappable placeholder theme, so locking real branding later is a token change rather than a rewrite.

**Target features:**
- **Design system foundation** — tokens (color, type scale, spacing, radius, elevation) as the single source of truth, component variants built on them, and the scaffold residue cleaned up (`Create Next App` metadata; the dead `--font-sans` reference in `@theme inline` that silently falls every `font-sans` back to a browser default)
- **Multi-theme mechanism** — named themes previewable side by side, so several candidate brand directions can be compared before one is locked
- **Booker flow polish** — home/search, listing detail, availability calendar, checkout, confirmation, my bookings, group invite/RSVP, open-capacity pass picker; with permission to reshape layout and information hierarchy on search results, listing detail, and checkout
- **Host tooling polish** — dashboard, listings + create/edit wizard, availability editor, requests inbox, host bookings, earnings/payouts
- **Auth & profile polish** — login, signup, forgot/reset password, profile
- **Transactional email shell** — a shared branded layout over the existing thin Resend sends
- **Image crop/framing UI** — avatar cropper + non-destructive cover-frame preview (promoted from backlog 999.2; UI-SPEC already written)

**Two net-new capability items** ride alongside the polish work in their own phases, with their own REQ-IDs, because research established they are *not* polish (they change what the product can do, not how it looks):
- **Search-results map** — a map view on `/` alongside the result list. Needs a bbox query parameter the two-stage PostGIS search does not take today, clustering, marker↔card sync, and its own a11y story.
- **Availability "copy to all"** — copying one day's operating hours across weekdays in the host availability editor.

**Placeholder, not branding.** Direction stays Airbnb-calm + coral accent (continuity with the Phase-3 sketch manifest and the shipped `--brand` token — cheapest to swap). Dark mode goes **light-only** (D-129, as amended): the 10 app-code `dark:` occurrences are rewritten into tokens; the 56 inside vendored shadcn components stay as provably-inert dead code, and the `.dark` token block stays dormant behind the theme system as a cheap future theme.

**Hard gates on every phase:** 320px-up responsive · keyboard operability + WCAG AA contrast/focus · designed loading, empty, and error states on every async surface · Playwright visual-regression baselines · **and GATE-NOREG** (D-134) — proof the *previously working* thing still works.

**Research established that several of these gates fail today, before any new work.** The full defect table with measured values and file:line is in `.planning/research/SUMMARY.md`; the load-bearing ones are that `--font-sans` is a self-referential CSS cycle (so the product has never rendered in its own typeface), that the coral CTA label, the success badge and the focus ring all fail the AA bar D-131 declares, and that Playwright's visual-regression gate is fail-open by default.

### Out of Scope

- Automated cost-splitting among group attendees (**GPAY-01**) — out of v1, but **explicitly on the roadmap, not "never"** (user product decision, 2026-07-25). v1 group bookings are organizer-pays + RSVP/headcount confirmation; that shell was built as a deliberate foundation for the paid/cost-split version rather than as standalone coordination.

- Native iOS/Android apps — v1 is a responsive web app; native apps considered after validation
- Multi-city / nationwide rollout at launch — launching in one city/region first to build marketplace liquidity (density beats breadth early)
- In-app messaging/chat between hosts and bookers — revisit after core booking flow proves out
- Reviews & ratings — valuable for trust but not required for the core transaction in v1
- Identity verification / background checks, smart-lock/access-code integration, add-ons at checkout, subscriptions/class packs, dynamic or tiered pricing — all held out of v1.0 (see `milestones/v1.0-REQUIREMENTS.md`); reasoning re-checked at the v1.0 close and still valid.

## Context

- **v1.0 MVP shipped 2026-08-11.** 9 phases / 107 plans / 227 tasks over 69 days and 755 commits. ~37.9k LOC of TypeScript across 211 files under `src/`, plus 135 test files; 1087 tests passing / 4 skipped / 0 failures and `tsc --noEmit` exit 0 at close. Stack as built: Next.js 16 App Router + React 19, PostgreSQL 18 with PostGIS and `btree_gist`, Drizzle ORM (migrations through `0025`), Better Auth, PayMongo, Inngest for crons, Resend for email, Tailwind v4 + shadcn/ui, Vitest + Playwright.
- **The hardest problems were the correctness ones, and they were solved at the database.** Double-booking is prevented by a GiST `EXCLUDE` constraint (exclusive listings) and an advisory-locked admissions counter (open-capacity listings) — never by an application-level query-then-insert. Payment state is driven by a signature-verified webhook, never the browser return.
- **What v1.0 has NOT proven:** real host payouts have never moved real money, and hosted host KYC has never been walked — both sales-gated PayMongo betas. Of the booker-side rails, only card and QR Ph have been individually hand-paid. See `STATE.md` § Deferred Items.
- **Greenfield project.** New build, no existing codebase. Working directory: `FitOut`.
- **Marketplace dynamics matter.** Two-sided marketplaces live or die on liquidity. Concentrating supply and demand in a single launch market is a deliberate strategy to ensure that when a booker searches, there are real, bookable spaces to find.
- **Demand-side first.** The booking experience (search → availability → reserve → pay) is the priority. Host tooling exists to make that experience possible, not the other way around.
- **Group bookings are the differentiator** versus generic booking tools, but not the core-value gate. v1 keeps them simple (invite + confirm headcount, organizer pays); richer mechanics (cost-splitting, per-spot payment) come later.
- **Real payments from day one.** v1 processes actual money — charging bookers, paying out hosts, and taking a platform commission — so the trust and correctness of the payment/payout flow is a first-class concern.

## Constraints

- **Platform**: Responsive web app (browser, desktop + mobile) — fastest path to a usable, testable product; native mobile deferred.
- **Payments**: Real money handling in v1 — charge booker, pay out host, platform takes a commission. The provider is **PayMongo** (D-20, adopted 2026-07-09; Stripe Connect was the original assumption and has no PH acquiring or QRPh). Host payout onboarding rides PayMongo Platforms / Linked Accounts. *Constraint discovered in v1.0 and now load-bearing:* the pieces of PayMongo that move money to hosts — `/v2` transfers, wallet enumeration, hosted KYC — are **sales-gated betas**, so shipping the payout half depends on a commercial conversation, not on code.
- **Launch market**: Single city/region at launch — features should not assume multi-region complexity, but data model should not preclude it later.
- **Availability correctness**: Bookings touch real time slots and real money — double-booking and stale-availability are unacceptable failure modes for the core value.
