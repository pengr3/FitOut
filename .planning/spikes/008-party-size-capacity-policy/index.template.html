“Fits your group” has two different meanings.

Choose a group size and policy. This experiment distinguishes a venue's published maximum capacity from its remaining places on a particular day.

Decision this exposes

Capacity can rule out a group that can never fit. But a drop-in space only has a truthful remaining places answer after a date is known. Should Phase 24 stop at capacity, or add date before it calls a space a fit?
