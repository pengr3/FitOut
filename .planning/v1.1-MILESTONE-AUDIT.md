# Milestone Audit: v1.1 — Front-End Polish & Placeholder Design System

**Status:** ⚠ `gaps_found`
**Audited:** 2026-08-31
**Phases in scope:** 10, 11, 12, 13, 13.1, 14, 15, 16, 16.1, 17, 17.1 (+ quick task `260831-ndc`)

## Verdict in one paragraph

The milestone did what it set out to do. The token contract exists, both themes ship, the gate
machinery is real and capable of failing, and every one of the eleven phases carries a
VERIFICATION.md that a verifier signed. Seven of eight cross-phase seams are wired, and all five
end-to-end flows trace complete. **One thing is genuinely broken:** a two-clause handoff from Phase 13
to Phase 15, recorded on both sides under decision D-78 and executed by neither. Separately, four
requirements are satisfied in the tree but were never ticked, which made the milestone look worse than
it is; correcting those is bookkeeping, not work.

## Requirements: 64/71 satisfied

79 requirements were defined. Eight (`SEARCH-06..09`, `MAP-01..04`) were deferred wholesale to backlog
999.3 by **D-141** before any plan was written — they are out of scope, not gaps. Of the 71 in scope:

| Disposition | Count | IDs |
|---|---|---|
| Satisfied and recorded | 60 | — |
| Satisfied but unticked (bookkeeping) | 4 | `DS-09`, `RESP-04`, `GATE-02`, `GATE-06` |
| Partial — blocked on a human or a business fact | 5 | `DS-11`, `STATE-05`, `TRUST-01`, `BFLOW-08`, `TRUST-05` |
| **Unsatisfied** | **2** | **`TRUST-02`, `TRUST-03`** |

The two unsatisfied IDs force `gaps_found` under the audit's FAIL gate.

### The four that are done but unrecorded

Phase 17's own verification says this in plain words:

> Flipping these four rows (and the four checkboxes) to `Complete` in both locations is process
> bookkeeping this verification surfaces but does not perform — it is the one remaining mechanical
> step for whoever closes this phase out.

Only `RESP-03` was ever flipped. `RESP-04`, `GATE-02` and `GATE-06` are verified true at `HEAD` and
still read `[ ] Pending` in both the checkbox list and the traceability table. `DS-09` is the same
story from a different phase: plan 17-05 converted the last five hand-rolled `h-11` buttons onto
`size="touch"` and flipped the adoption gate from advisory to blocking at `toBe(0)`, but its
frontmatter claimed `GATE-02` only, so `DS-09` was never ticked either.

**Consequence:** the ROADMAP progress table's note that "RESP-03/RESP-04/GATE-02/GATE-06 remain
Pending by design" is no longer true. They remain Pending by omission.

### The two that are genuinely open — the Phase 13 to 15 email handoff

This is the one finding that would make a "milestone complete" claim inaccurate.

**Decision D-78** forbade Phase 13 from touching the email shell and handed two clauses to Phase 15:

- **`TRUST-02`** — the booking reference must appear in the confirmation email's **subject line**.
- **`TRUST-03`** — the cancellation policy must be disclosed **in the confirmation email**, with
  concrete dates.

Phase 13 delivered both on-screen halves and un-ticked both IDs specifically because the email halves
were owed. The handoff is recorded in five separate Phase 13 artifacts, and Phase 14's research doc
repeats it. Phase 15 then shipped the email shell — and never received either ID.

Verified in the code. The subject, at `src/lib/email.ts:141`, carries the space title and nothing
else; the FIT- reference appears only in the body at `:137`. And the body itself, at
`src/lib/email.ts:136-138`, is a single sentence — "You're booked at {space} on {when}. Booking
reference {ref}." — with no cancellation policy, no percentage, and no date.

What makes this a dropped handoff rather than an accepted deferral: Phase 15's plan **15-03**
(commit `85ceeda`) rewrote this exact function from a hand-built HTML string to `renderEmail({...})`
for EMAIL-01. It was editing the two lines that needed changing. Its frontmatter reads
`requirements: [EMAIL-01]`, and a grep for `TRUST-02` or `TRUST-03` across the entire Phase 15
directory returns **zero files**. Nobody decided to defer this; it fell between two phases.

REQUIREMENTS.md is honest about the gap — both IDs still read `[ ]` with the email half named as
outstanding. The documentation is accurate. The work is missing.

## Tech debt

Roughly **85 deferred items** across the milestone's phase ledgers. The two that matter at milestone
scale:

**1. Phase 17's escalate-class ledger — ~22 of 28 entries still open.** `17-D1/D2/D3` were closed by
Phase 17.1 and `17-D28` by quick task `260831-9qx`; the sticky-bar clearance band and the PayMongo
interception seam were addressed by `17.1-03..07`. The remainder were filed for PM review and never
dispositioned.

**2. The milestone's closing proof is not enforced by CI.** None of Phase 17's seven Playwright specs
run in any CI job — only `e2e/price-parity.spec.ts` and `--project=visual` do. This is **D-24**, a
pre-existing project-wide decision that predates Phase 17, but Phase 17's own verifier names it as the
concrete reason the GATE-02 gap survived four independent review passes: the assertion worked exactly
as designed and nothing was running it. Every gate this milestone declares is real; most of them are
only run by hand.

Also open: Phase 14's nine GATE-VRT baselines were never generated (`--project=visual` does not exist
on win32), and Phase 15's WR-05 is unfixed in the tree — `scripts/send-email-previews.ts:226`
allow-lists only `RESEND_API_KEY` and `EMAIL_FROM`, so `BETTER_AUTH_URL` is unset and two of the 23
preview sends carry a dead CTA. That is a harness artefact, but it will grade the wrong thing if the
email walk runs before it is fixed.
