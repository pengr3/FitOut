## Phase 7 UX Design Thread (exploratory — feed into `/gsd-discuss-phase 7`)

Discussed at end of session; capture these so they're not lost:

1. **Feeds don't live-update (needs refresh).** Approve/decline and new-request only refresh the *acting* user's view (`revalidatePath`); the other party must reload. Architectural (Server Components, no WebSocket/SSE). Backstopped by email (`sendRequestApproved` / `sendNewRequestToHost`). **User concern:** a booker who doesn't refresh never learns they were approved / need to pay → forgets → booking lapses. **Wants a real, unmissable notification.**
2. **KEY INSIGHT — window length ⇄ notification strength are coupled.** A short payment window makes a missed notification *fatal*. So **fix notifications first**, then a short window is safe.
3. **Payment window (currently flat 24h).** User leans toward a **fast 15-min** window. Tension: request-to-book approval is async (host latency), so "15 min from approval" punishes the booker for a slow host. **Better:** start the booker's pay clock on **acknowledgment** (when they open the approval), not on the host's click; cap the outer hold. Models on the table — **A** Forgiving (long, capped at session start), **B** Live-fast (15-min from approval), **C** Acknowledgment-triggered (15-min from booker opening). **Recommended: C + a strong notification stack** (TanStack Query poll + toast + visible countdown + pre-expiry reminder + graceful one-click re-request on expiry).
4. **REAL CORRECTNESS GAP (not just polish).** Both `APPROVAL_PAYMENT_WINDOW_HOURS` and `APPROVAL_SLA_HOURS` are set as flat `now() + 24h` with **no cap at `booking.starts_at`** (`src/app/actions/host-requests.ts:175`). A 2pm approval of a 5pm session holds/allows-pay until 2pm next day — past the session; the confirm path also doesn't check `starts_at > now()`. **Fix:** `expires_at = LEAST(now() + window, starts_at)` and refuse approve/confirm once the session is imminent/past. Stakes bounded (pay-on-approval = no charge until pay, D-63) — a lapse costs a slot, not money. Constants are env-tunable in `src/lib/payments/config.ts` (D-64).
