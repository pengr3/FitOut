<objective>
Make the ordinary booking detail page complete on every status — which is the precondition that makes
the confirmation moment safe to decay.

Purpose: D-60 rests on one claim — *"nothing important may live ONLY in the moment; everything it
states is repeated on the ordinary detail page."* 13-UI-SPEC turns that claim into an acceptance
criterion rather than a hope: render the detail WITHOUT `?paid=1` and assert every fact is present.
This plan is what makes that assertion pass, so it runs before the moment is built. TRUST-01's first
clause — *"status plus what it means"* — is the one most likely to be waved through, so 13-UI-SPEC
writes the ten sentences out as a table; this plan ships them. D-90 corrects the one place the original
decision was wrong: request-to-book is pay-on-approval, so a `requested` booker has **not** been
charged, and telling them otherwise is false and worse than saying nothing.

Output: one shell rendered eight ways, the ten status meanings, the reference on every status, the
policy disclosure on the detail, the cancelled branch's money statement, an owner-safe not-found page,
and the two RTL proofs.
</objective>

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| booking id → page render | The id is guessable-shaped in a URL; the owner gate is the only thing between a stranger and a name, an address and an amount |
| not-found copy → existence oracle | Distinguishable copy for "missing" vs "not yours" leaks whether a booking exists |
| status meaning copy → the booker's belief about their money | A `requested` booker told they were charged is a false money statement |
