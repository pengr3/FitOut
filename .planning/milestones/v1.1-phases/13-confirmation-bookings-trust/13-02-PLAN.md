| server → client component | `BookingReference` is a Client Component; anything it receives is in the bundle and in the DOM |
| app → outbound mailto | The support subject line leaves the app carrying whatever is interpolated into it |
| business identity → user | A published support address nobody reads is a spoof of the business (D-26/D-64) |
