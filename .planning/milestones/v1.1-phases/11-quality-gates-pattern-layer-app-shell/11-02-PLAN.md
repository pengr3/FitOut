reflex); the measured counts 92/30/63/23; and the **scope rule** from the UI-SPEC — a `data-testid` is added
ONLY where a role or label query cannot express the target, and the shipped 92 `getByRole` / 30
`getByLabel` selectors are never converted to test ids.
