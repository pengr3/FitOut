<objective>
Remove every remaining 50%-alpha focus ring in the repository and pin the result with a source-scan gate.

Purpose: DS-05 cannot be closed by the token value alone. `ring-ring/50` compiles to a 50% `color-mix`,
and composited over white a `#555555` ring measures 2.32:1; the lightest neutral that reaches 3:1 at 50%
alpha still fails on `--muted` at 2.93:1. This is arithmetic, not preference — the `/50` must go, and it
must go from all 13 sites, not just the vendored ones a linter happens to reach.

Output: 12 files carrying the canonical recipe from plan 10-06, and one test that keeps the count at zero.
</objective>
