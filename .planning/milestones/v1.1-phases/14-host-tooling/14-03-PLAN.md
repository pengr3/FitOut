<objective>
Make the deadline the loudest thing on a request row, keep the row terminal, and stop a refusal from
arriving in a toast that is gone on refresh.

Purpose: HFLOW-01 asks for a scannable triage queue whose SLA countdown leads and whose only actions are
approve and decline. D-146 makes "loudest" a measurement rather than an adjective, D-144 closes Phase 11's
row-navigability question by keeping the row terminal, and D-145 confirms the irreversible half and leaves
the reversible half at one press. The refusal path is the one real behaviour change: on success the row
revalidates away and a toast is the only possible report, but on refusal the row is still on screen and the
sentence explaining why belongs on it.

Output: an opt-in emphasis on the shared countdown that changes nothing for the booker, a request row whose
first line is the deadline, a decline that confirms through the app's one overlay, and a refusal that
survives a refresh.
</objective>
