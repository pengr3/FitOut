Turn the inbox into a triage queue: the deadline first on the desktop table, the shared shell and header on
the page, and a browser measurement that makes "loudest" a fact.

Purpose: HFLOW-01's own words put the SLA countdown first, and the shipped column order puts it fifth of
six — the opposite. D-154's "no new information architecture" rule is scoped to the bookings table; the inbox
is explicitly asked to lead with the deadline. And D-146 is a hierarchy instruction, so it is written as a
comparison a browser can perform rather than an adjective a reviewer can agree with.

Output: a re-ordered table, a page and plate sharing one shell and one header, and a Playwright spec that
compares computed type sizes inside a real row at three widths.
</objective>
