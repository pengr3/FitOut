clock.

to action. D-140 chose real sessions over a tile grid because a host with three sessions today must see WHO
is coming without a second click. D-141 makes "today" venue-local from the database clock, D-142 keeps a
quiet day useful, and D-143 demotes the greeting to a title. The components exist; this is the composition,
the read wiring, the plate and the proof.

Output: a dashboard whose first block is today's sessions, a plate shaped like it, and an end-to-end spec
that catches a fourth writing of the pending-request predicate.
</objective>
