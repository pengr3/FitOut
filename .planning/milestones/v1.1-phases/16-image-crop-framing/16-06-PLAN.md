<objective>
Ship CROP-02: the honest half of the listing-cover problem.

Purpose: there is no single cover ratio to crop to. The cover renders 16:9 on the listing hero and 4:3
on both card surfaces, so any destructive cover crop bakes in the wrong framing for at least one of them
and then `object-cover` crops it a second time (999.2 § Source of truth, finding 4). The correct fix is a
stored focal region delivered per-surface — a delivery-pipeline phase, explicitly out of scope. So this
phase shows the host both cuts and changes nothing.

Output: four copy literals, a server-safe presentational component, its mount, and a jsdom spec.

**Delta-8 reverses 999.2 Open Question 6 and DELETES its mandatory ratio-agreement test from the contract.**
Q6's premise — "no shared ratio constant exists" — was overturned by Phase 12: `measurements.ts:50` exports
`RESULT_CARD_MEDIA = "aspect-[4/3]"` and `:365` exports `MOSAIC_ASPECT = "aspect-[16/9]"`, and
`photo-gallery.tsx` already reads the class and records why: *"The class IS the constant, so there is one
spelling."* Importing gives structurally what that test was reaching for, and a test asserting a constant
equals itself is noise. **Do not write it.** The shipped render surfaces are still not edited.
</objective>
