<objective>
Build the surface this phase exists for.

Purpose: the operator's sentence, 2026-08-10, is still the standard — *"it just inserted the photo without
confirming or adjusting the zoom and whatever."* This dialog is the confirming and the adjusting.

Output: `src/components/profile/image-crop-dialog.tsx`, plus its live-region declaration.

**Two things this plan deliberately does NOT do, each with the plan that does it.**

1. **The mask ring and the scrim are left at the library's own values here** (a 1px `rgba(255,255,255,.5)`
   border and a 50%-black `box-shadow` scrim). Delta-6 wants a 2px `--background` ring and a
   `--foreground`-at-55% scrim, but RESEARCH §A5 / R7 measured that the library injects its stylesheet
   UNLAYERED into `document.head` while Tailwind v4 utilities live in a cascade layer — and unlayered beats
   layered — so the `classes.cropAreaClassName` route is the right INTENT and is not guaranteed to win.
   R3 requires a real-browser `getComputedStyle` measurement to decide the route BEFORE the diluted-token
   inventory row commits, and the stage does not exist in any browser until plans 16-10/16-11 wire it to
   `/profile`. **Plan 16-13 measures it and lands the winning route together with (or without) its inventory
   row.** Shipping a guessed route plus its inventory row here is exactly what R3 forbids.
2. **It performs no upload and knows nothing about Cloudinary.** Props are geometry and copy; it returns a
   `Blob` to its caller. `AvatarField` (plan 16-10) owns the file, the guards and the action.

**The most likely first-try bug in the phase, named so it is not debugged twice (R12).**
`.reactEasyCrop_Container` is `position: absolute` with all four insets at 0 and NO intrinsic size. It sizes
to its offset parent. If the stage wrapper is not `relative`, the cropper escapes to the nearest positioned
ancestor — which inside `ResponsiveDialog` is `DialogContent` (`fixed`, `ui/dialog.tsx:73`) — and fills the
entire overlay.
