# Phase 13.1 Plan 04: Retire the Lapsed Hold's Checkout Session Summary

**When a hold lapses, the booker's ability to pay now lapses with it — a 5-minute singleton cron probes every recently-lapsed hold's checkout session and retires it at PayMongo, proven not by a call count but by PayMongo's own record reporting a real `sk_test_` session `active` before the sweep and `expired` after, with the `paid` case never sent to expire at all and the lapsed row deliberately left `pending` so plan 02's reconciler still owns it.**

## Why D-113 is true WITHOUT 13.1-05

This is the plan's central claim, so it is argued rather than asserted.

- **The sweep's reach is a superset of the three inline wirings', not a subset.** `queryRetirableSessions` selects on `status IN ('pending','approved') AND expires_at <= now()` — every hold that lapses, regardless of whether anything ever visits it. The three reclaim paths 13.1-05 will wire fire only when *something else happens*: another booker places an overlapping hold (`units.ts` ×2), or the request-expiry cron flips an `approved` row.
- **The dominant case has no reclaim path at all.** A lapsed `pending` instant-book hold on a slot nobody else wants is flipped by NOTHING. `request-expiry.ts` selects only `requested`/`approved`. The lazy read predicates free the slot for *display*, so the system looks healthy while the row sits `pending` forever and its session stays payable forever. A plan that wired only the enumerated reclaims would have satisfied the letter of the enumeration and left this wide open.
- **Nothing here depends on 13.1-05 landing.** `RETIRE_INLINE_LIMIT` and `retireCheckoutsForBookings` exist and are tested, but the sweep does not call them — it uses `retireCheckoutForBooking` per row under `RETIRE_BATCH_LIMIT`. Delete the batch export and the guarantee is unchanged.
- **The one honest residual:** a hold that lapses while this cron is DOWN for more than ~25 minutes leaves the lookback window unswept and its session stays payable. That is the price of D-112's no-migration constraint (a marker column would remove the window). It is bounded — D-108's retained gone-slot backstop still governs a payment that lands afterwards — and it is the strongest argument for 13.1-05's inline wirings, which retire at the moment of reclaim with no window at all. Stated at the constant's declaration, not hidden.
