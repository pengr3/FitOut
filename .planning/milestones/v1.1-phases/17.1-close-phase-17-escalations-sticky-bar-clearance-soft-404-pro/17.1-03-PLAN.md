<objective>
Measure the 640-1023px band on `/listings/[id]/book`, then leave a permanent instrument behind that
catches the next override by the pixel.

Purpose: `sm:py-12` beats the unvariant `pb-20` (Tailwind v4 emits variant utilities in a later
`@media` block), so **`STICKY_BAR_CLEARANCE` has never been in effect at or above 640px on either
route**. The listing route was repaired as a side effect of `4636206`'s wrapper move. The checkout
route still carries the overridden spelling, giving a **64px** fixed bar over **48px** of bottom
padding across **640-1023px** — both bars are `lg:hidden`, so a bar renders up to 1023px.

**Whether a control is actually occluded there is unmeasured**, and no instrument in the suite drives
that band: `mobile-booker-path.spec.ts` runs `PHONE {375,812}`, `FLOOR {320,568}` and
`DESKTOP {1280,900}` only. That is an instrument gap as well as a possible code defect, and it is
RESP-03's literal subject — *"with the sticky bar present"* — across a 384px-wide band.

This plan does **not** repair anything. It measures, and it builds the assertion that will hold the
repair honest. Plan 17.1-04 lands the repair, which per D-04 lands **regardless of the probe's
outcome** — only its shape is conditional.

Output: two recorded transcripts, and a band case that reds today.
</objective>
