padding — 16px short of the bar's own height, against a **declared 80px**. That is `[17-D10]`'s shape

verified base-layer order, and **no `sm:pb-*` exists anywhere in the tree to have measured it**. Task 1
measures it. A model of the sorter is the thing that was wrong last time.

prediction handed forward, one disposed probe.
