## The repair, and why its shape was the whole question

`src/app/listings/[id]/book/page.tsx:521`:

```
- cn("mx-auto w-full max-w-4xl px-4 py-8 sm:py-12", STICKY_BAR_CLEARANCE)
+ cn("mx-auto w-full max-w-4xl px-4 py-8 sm:py-12", STICKY_BAR_CLEARANCE, "sm:pb-20 lg:pb-12")
```

`STICKY_BAR_CLEARANCE` stays imported, stays the unvariant term and stays the declared value. The
repair makes it **true** rather than replacing it — which is the point, because `[17-D10]`'s own
argument is that *"deleting a shipped protection because it is currently inert is exactly the move that
breaks the day the layout changes"*, and a protection that is declared but never in effect is that same
defect from the other side.

Measured on the live route (a resolved checkout with a real hold), both themes, before → after:

| width | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| 320 / 639 | 80px | **80px** — unchanged |
| 640 / 768 / 1023 | **48px** against a 64px bar | **80px** |
| 1024 / 1280 | 48px | **48px** — unchanged, so `checkout-1280` cannot move |

The last control's clearance across the band goes from **4px** (court) / **5px** (grove) back to
**36px / 37px** — the floor's own figure. `§ P2` called that 4px *"not a passing grade, it is the
finding restated"*; the ninefold collapse is reversed rather than tolerated.

**Shape B was available and was not taken, for two costs that were priced before the choice.** Moving
the clearance onto `book/layout.tsx`'s wrapper would have (a) reversed `[17-D10]`'s recorded placement
decision — must-escalate under 17-UI-SPEC § Remediation — and (b) moved `checkout-320-court.png` by
+32px, a baseline plan 17.1-07's `§7a` prediction would then have to absorb. Shape A costs neither, and
the reason behind the recorded decision is untouched by the defect: checkout renders no footer
(`shell.spec.ts:1233` pins "0 footers" on a live checkout, and it passed this session), so `<main>`
**is** this document's bottom. **The defect was the class, never the element.**

  never been in effect at or above 640px, so there was no unconditional 80px to defend. `grep -c

  off and why, the band, `lg:pb-12`'s coupling to `sm:py-12` (they move together, and it is a literal

- **`mobile-booker-path.spec.ts` block (m)** closed by quoting the 320px 36px clearance as the route's
  whole story, with DRIVE 2 offered as its watched red. Both halves are true *at 320px and nowhere
  else*. The placement half was kept; the measurement half now carries `§ P2`'s and `§ P4`'s band
  numbers and says plainly that DRIVE 2 ran at the floor, inside the band where the clearance already
  worked, which is why it never saw this.

## Decisions Made

- **Shape A, on a measurement rather than a preference.** The plan made a refutation of A2 a
  stop-and-escalate; A2 held, so the escalation was not needed and Shape B's two costs were not
  incurred. Had it failed, `§ P4` row 4 (the `pt`/`pb` split) was already measured as an *in-place*
  answer, so even the escalation would have arrived with an alternative that did not reverse a decision.
- **`lg:pb-12` is a literal and carries the reason at the class site.** It hands back exactly the 48px
  `sm:py-12` supplies, at the first width where no bar renders. It cannot be a constant: Tailwind v4
  scans source **text**, so a `lg:`-prefixed template of some constant is never emitted and would
  silently resolve to nothing — the same in-the-list / not-on-the-box failure this whole plan closes.
  No new spacing value was added to `measurements.ts`; that is must-escalate.
- **The band probe's `VIEWPORTS` array was extended by two rows before its final drive** (`639×568`,
  `1280×900`) so the after-reading covers all seven widths rather than the five `§ P2` happened to
  carry. Its md5 moved from `5cef0225…` to `82b4ed20…`; `§ P2` is unaffected because its transcript is
  recorded verbatim and is not re-derived from the file. Stated in `§ P4` rather than left for a reader
  to notice — and the file was deleted minutes later.
- **`768×1024` stays the viewport where the occlusion question cannot be asked.** `maxScroll 0`; its
  `main pb` moved 48 → 80 with every other band row, and the permanent case still drives the heights
  that scroll, for the reason `§ P2` recorded.
- **RESP-03 is not marked complete and `[17-D10]` is closed with a correction, not a checkmark.**
  Advancing a requirement and closing it are different claims, and this phase's whole subject is the
  difference.
