<objective>
Measure RESP-04's rendered half — the question a source scan structurally cannot answer.

Purpose: plan 17-03 proved that **no file** renders a viewport-conditional JSX branch and that `src/`
holds exactly one `matchMedia` call site. Neither fact proves the *rendered* consequence. A component
tree can be free of `{isMobile ? … : …}` and still mount a client island twice, or hide one copy with
`opacity-0` instead of `hidden` and leave two navigation landmarks in the accessibility tree. The
assertion RESP-04 actually needs is a **count over the document at three widths**, and it must be
"1 in the document", not "1 visible": `[data-testid]` resolves against `display: none` nodes exactly
as it resolves against painted ones, which is precisely why the count is the right assertion and
"is visible" is not.

Two of the six named surfaces could not be counted at all before plan 17-03 — the search-results
region and the availability calendar had no identifying container id, only `skeleton-calendar`, which
is the *fallback* and answers "is it loading" rather than "is there one calendar".

Output: one spec, six surface families × three widths, plus the landmark count.
</objective>
