<objective>
Close RESP-03's two uninstrumented clauses — **B: with the sticky bar present**, and **C: no price,
countdown or label wraps** — in the file that already owns the sticky bar's geometry.

Purpose: `scrollWidth <= clientWidth` is green on a `₱1,234.00` that has wrapped onto a second line
inside a fixed-height bar. The text is clipped, not overflowing, and the harness reports success.
RESP-03's literal wording is therefore two assertions and only the second exists. Separately, no gate
asserts the bar is *there* — the 320px sweep measures pages on which the bar may have silently stopped
rendering, which makes clause A's green a statement about a page that is missing the thing clause B is
about.

**Decision — where the sticky-bar clause lives.** 17-PATTERNS § Decision Point offers a block inside
`e2e/overflow-320.spec.ts` or a new `e2e/sticky-bar.spec.ts`. This plan takes a third option and
records why: **extend `e2e/mobile-booker-path.spec.ts`**, which already declares `BAR`
(`[data-testid="booking-sticky-bar"]`, :198), `CHECKOUT_BAR` (:701), `BAR_HEIGHT_PX = 64` (:184),
`TOUCH_TARGET_PX = 44` (:187), `TOLERANCE_PX = 1` (:190), `FLOOR = {320,568}` (:178),
`DESKTOP = {1280,900}` (:181), `boxOf()` (:233), `reachableBar()` (:248) and the seeded listing both
bars need. Both alternatives would retype at least four of those, which is the drift the
import-the-constant-never-retype-it rule exists to stop; and `overflow-320.spec.ts` is already 2,092
lines and is this phase's serialisation bottleneck across three other plans.

Output: one shared helper, and clauses B and C measured on the two pages that carry a bar.
</objective>
