## The finding: `[17-D26]` — four baselines with a one-day shelf life

Cropping `listing-detail-1280` at x 130–530, y 1600–1800 shows what the 173 actually is:

```
OLD baseline (minted 2026-08-26)          NEW (regenerated 2026-08-30, Asia/Manila)
  23 24 25 [26] 27 28 29                    23 24 25  26  27 28 29    <- 26..29 now greyed out
  30 31  1  2  3  4  5                     [30] 31  1  2  3  4  5     <- today-ring moved here
```

`src/app/listings/[id]/(detail)/page.tsx` passes `todayDate={todayLocal}` at three call sites, and
`availability-calendar.tsx:500` takes `todayDate ?? initialDate` — so `todayStart` is the **real
venue-local today**, driving the opening month, the `--muted` today-ring and
`disabled={[{ before: todayStart }, …]}`.

**The row's own docblock believes it is protected and is wrong.** It says `?date=` *"IS WHAT MAKES
THIS BASELINE DETERMINISTIC"*. Measured: it pins the **selected day and the hour grid** and nothing
else — the grid still opens on **today's** month, which is why the crop above is **August**, not the
September the parameter names.

**Timeline, measured:** the previous baselines were minted by dispatch `32925834322` at
`2026-08-26T03:16Z` (Manila 11:16, Aug 26) and confirmed green by `ci` run `32945807603` at `08:03Z`
**the same Manila day**. The next rollover broke them. `gate-visual` has been red on `dev` since
**2026-08-27** for this reason alone.

**⚠ The four regenerated calendar baselines now encode "today = 30 August 2026" and will be red at
the next Manila rollover.** The regeneration re-pinned the defect to a different day; it did not fix
it and does not pretend to. The cheapest correct fix — set `needsClock` on `listingDetailDrive`,
`sheetDrive` and `collisionDrive`, the mechanism `checkoutDrive` already uses and `surfaces.spec.ts`
already wires — is written out in `[17-D26]`, together with why doing it here was refused.
