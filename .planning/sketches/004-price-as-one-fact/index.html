004 · price

The claim under test (BFLOW-04): "The price breakdown a booker sees in the listing rail is visually the same component at checkout, so they recognise it as the same fact." That is a recognisability claim — the only way to judge it is to put the two surfaces side by side and look.

What makes it buildable: the rail is already fed a server-computed AllInTable keyed by hour count, exact to the centavo, with zero client arithmetic. Itemising it is a matter of widening that table to carry {space, fee, total} per key — not of moving a computation into the browser, which GATE-05 fails the build over.

Also under test: "Est." is gone. Both surfaces say Total. Click the ⓘ next to the service fee — that popover is the "explains itself on demand" half.

✓ What differs, deliberately: the rail adds the rate headline above and a "held for 15 minutes" promise below; checkout adds the live countdown and the pay action. The breakdown block between them is byte-identical — same rows, same order, same weights, same tabular-nums, same word Total. That is the component BFLOW-04 is asking for.

⚠ FOUND WHILE BUILDING THIS — two different numbers wear "/hr" on the same panel. Look at the rail: the headline says ₱714/hr and the run line four rows below says ₱680/hr × 2 hours. Both are correct and they are not the same rate.

D-75 makes every browsing surface quote the all-in rate so the number never goes up between browsing and paying — that is ₱714. But price-breakdown.tsx's run line is built from hourlyRateCents, the host's raw space rate — that is ₱680, and it has to be, because the fee is disclosed on its own line directly underneath and quoting ₱714 there would charge the fee twice on screen.

This is new, and it is caused by the decision we just made. Today the two never meet: the headline lives on the listing page and the itemised run line lives at checkout. Moving the breakdown into the rail puts them 60px apart for the first time.

Three ways out, none free: (1) drop the rail's rate headline entirely once the breakdown is there — the Total is the number that matters and the rate is on the card that got them here; (2) label the run line "Space rate ₱680/hr × 2 hours" so the two are visibly different quantities; (3) keep both and accept it. Worth deciding here rather than discovering in review.

△ The gap this phase is closing. Same number, ₱1,428.00, in two visually unrelated containers — a bordered inset labelled Est. on the left, an itemised panel labelled Total on the right. Nothing about the left tells the booker the right will agree with it. And "Est." is doing real harm: the figure is exact by construction, so the word undersells a guarantee the system actually makes.

One copy line does not survive the move, and it is worth catching before the plans are written. price-breakdown.tsx ends with "Includes our service fee. You'll pay this now." On the rail, "you'll pay this now" is false — no hold exists yet, and the next click creates one rather than charging. The line has to become surface-conditional.
