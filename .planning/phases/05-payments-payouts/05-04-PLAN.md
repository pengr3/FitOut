## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-05-17 | Spoofing / Tampering | Forged or replayed payment/refund webhook confirming or refunding a booking | mitigate | Reuse the constant-time Paymongo-Signature verify over the raw body → 400; `paymongo_event` dedupe → replay is a 200 no-op; test both (Tasks 1/3) |
| T-05-18 | Tampering (privilege) | A smuggled `status`/amount body field driving the confirm/refund | mitigate | State DERIVED from `event.data.attributes.type`; the confirm reads `reference_number`, the amount from the frozen booking — never a client body field (Tasks 1-3) |
| T-05-19 | Availability / financial | Charging a booker for a slot that's gone with no money back | mitigate | 0-row confirm → auto-refund (refundable rails) or operator-alert (QRPh/UBP) — never silent retention (D-58, Task 2) |
| T-05-20 | Tampering | Re-imposing the expiry guard causing a paid booking to fail to confirm | mitigate | Confirm on `status='pending'` alone; the EXCLUDE (not the TTL) prevents a double-confirm (Pitfall 4, Task 1) |
| T-05-21 | Info-disclosure | PayMongo/refund error leaking secrets into logs/response | mitigate | Structured, PII-free alert lines; generic 200/400 responses; secret stays server-only |
| T-05-22 | Tampering (financial) | Double-confirming the same slot for two bookers during payment | mitigate | GiST EXCLUDE is the double-book authority; the loser gets 0 rows → auto-refund/alert (Task 2 double-book test) |
</threat_model>
