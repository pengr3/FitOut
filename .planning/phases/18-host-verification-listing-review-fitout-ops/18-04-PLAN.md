<objective>
Close all three surfaces through which an unreviewed listing can be read by the public — including the
one nobody had named.

Purpose: D-208 says a pending listing is hidden. Search closes by construction once 18-03 lands, and
the public page closes through the shipped soft-404. But `listingCardFacts` runs its own copy of the
published check, so `/listings/[id]/opengraph-image` would keep rendering a real card with the
listing's title, space type, city and rate for anyone who requests it — while the page itself 404s.
That is a public read of an unreviewed listing's contents, on a route explicitly designed to be
fetched by scrapers, and it is invisible in a browser.

Output: one expression with a required third parameter and three call sites, and the tests that pin
each of them.
</objective>
