<objective>
Give a booker one true sentence about what FitOut actually did — and make it structurally impossible
to say it about a listing nobody checked.

Purpose: Success Criterion 6 asks for a badge that states what FitOut checked and never implies we
inspected the space. D-206 means there IS no document and no vendor, so the honest fact is small: a
person at FitOut looked at this account and this listing. D-212 means the badge must never appear on
the grandfathered catalogue — which is the majority of the live catalogue on day one. The safe way to
guarantee that is not a careful conditional; it is to reduce the two statuses to one boolean in the
RSC so the client never receives the distinction at all.

Output: one pure predicate, one badge that takes a boolean, two booker surfaces, and a
deliberately-strengthened trust-signal gate.
</objective>
