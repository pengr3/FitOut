<objective>
Give ops the escalation lever — cancel the bookings on a confirmed-fake listing and put the bookers'
money back — without letting it silently do the wrong thing at any of the five places the host-cancel
machinery is host-specific.

Purpose: ENF-03. `cancelBookingAsHost` cannot be ridden by an ops actor unchanged, and **two of its
five host-specific parts fail SILENTLY rather than loudly**: the `starts_at > now()` guard would
quietly protect exactly the bookings most worth undoing, and `cancelled_by = 'host'` would blame the
host for FitOut's own decision. A third — the notification — would tell a defrauded booker their host
cancelled on them.

Output: one named constant, one impact read, one sibling action with five documented forks, and the
test that measures every one of them.

⚠ **This plan does NOT settle the D-236 conflict.** It implements the PM's answer and isolates it so
one line flips it. Raising the conflict is plan 18-14's job and it leads the phase summary.
</objective>
