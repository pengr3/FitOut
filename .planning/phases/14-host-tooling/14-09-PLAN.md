<objective>
Turn the wizard's step rail from decoration into navigation, without moving the one accent line a committed
gate counts.

Purpose: HFLOW-02 asks for back-navigation from a clickable step rail. D-148 scopes it to VISITED steps
below the current one, because each step autosaves independently but a first-time host who free-jumps can
land on a step whose earlier answers are blank — and the occupancy fork changes which steps even exist. That
same fork is why visited-ness is keyed rather than indexed. And D-151 records that the truthful step count is
already true, so it is planned here as a no-regression obligation with a test.

Output: markers that are controls when they should be and inert when they should not, an accent narrowed from
two states to one without moving a pinned count, and a test that walks the mode switch the index-keyed
version would fail.
</objective>
