<objective>
Draw the week the host is typing, and make the drawing and the announcement the same fact.

Purpose: D-152's whole point is that a host who types nine in the morning where they meant nine at night sees
the bar jump from the bottom third of the column to the top third — a SHAPE change, legible with no scale to
read, and visible before a save round-trip. D-153 keeps that honest for a screen-reader user by deriving the
sentence from the same call. D-155 spends the exemption the pattern layer held open for exactly this
structural pass.

Output: a strip whose bars are decoration and whose sentences are the meaning, mounted above a day editor
that no longer opens a raw card, with the two inventory rows that permitted it removed.
</objective>
