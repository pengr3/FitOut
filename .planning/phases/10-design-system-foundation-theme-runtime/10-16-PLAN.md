<objective>
Build the one real screen this phase ships: two themes rendered side by side in nested `[data-theme]`
subtrees, on real components fed by static fixtures, gated out of production.

Purpose: success criterion 3 is that a brand direction can be judged on real screens rather than on
swatches, so the section order puts real components first and swatches last. And THEME-04 is the assertion
that `@theme inline` is still doing its job: drop `inline` and the `<html>`-level switch keeps working
while every nested subtree silently renders the root theme — which is exactly why that failure is easy to
miss and why this page exists.

Output: the preview route, its fixture module, and the compiled-CSS proof that nesting really resolves.
</objective>
