<objective>
Migrate every surface off the literals that a theme swap cannot reach: the 14 arbitrary `text-[NNpx]`
values, the 14 shadow call sites and the 23 raw z-index values.

Purpose: Tailwind's default `shadow-*` utilities compile to literal values, not `var()` references, so a
surface carrying `shadow-md` is frozen no matter what a theme declares. An arbitrary `text-[28px]` is
frozen the same way. These are the sites that would silently make the grove proof a lie.

Output: 51 mechanical edits across 10 files plus the shadow and z sites, and two extended tests that keep
the count at zero.
</objective>
