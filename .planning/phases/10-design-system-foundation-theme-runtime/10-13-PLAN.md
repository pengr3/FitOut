| app z scale → third-party widget internals | A vendor with its own z-index (Leaflet at 1000) is outside the scale's authority |

The gate pins exact counts on both sides — 21 mapped and 2 surviving `z-0` in a named file — plus a >200-file floor, and the executor observes it going red on a reinstated `z-50`. |
| T-10-50 | Tampering | third-party z-index (Leaflet at 1000) | accept | Out of scope for this phase; recorded as a Phase 18 rule — wrap the vendor in a CSS stacking context rather than inflating the app scale. Lowering the portal layer from 50 to 30 does not change the relationship to Leaflet, which already sat above both. |
