    The mapping to apply, adjusting per site after reading it: `bg-white` on a page surface becomes
    `bg-background`, and on a raised surface becomes `bg-card`. `text-white` on a `--primary` fill becomes
    `text-primary-foreground`, and on a brand fill becomes `text-brand-foreground`. A numbered neutral at
    the 50/100 end (`bg-zinc-50`, `bg-neutral-100`, `bg-gray-50`) becomes `bg-muted`. A numbered neutral at
    the 800/900 end used as text becomes `text-foreground`; at the 500/600 end used as text it becomes
    `text-muted-foreground`. A numbered neutral at the 200 end used as a divider becomes `border-border`.
    A numbered green becomes `--success` only if it is a status signal, and otherwise becomes a neutral —
    green is not decoration (DS-10).

    `bg-foreground/50` so the scrim reads from the theme's ink rather than from an absolute black — that

    scans the vendored tree with no exemption, and the measured exposure for this rule is exactly one fix.

    If a rewrite produces a foreground/background pairing that is not already in
    `src/lib/design/contrast-pairs.ts`, add the row with its bar and note and re-run
    `npm run test:design -- contrast`; a pairing that fails its bar means the wrong token was chosen, not
