<objective>
Turn the coral accent into a CVA variant, add the 44px `touch` size, and remove every 50%-alpha focus
ring in the repository.

Purpose: DS-05 cannot be closed by the token value alone. `ring-ring/50` compiles to a 50% `color-mix`,
and composited over white a `#555555` ring measures 2.32:1; the lightest neutral that reaches 3:1 at 50%
alpha still fails on `--muted` at 2.93:1. This is arithmetic, not preference — the `/50` must go. And
exactly 20 of the 29 `bg-brand` source lines are on a `<Button>`; converting all 29 breaks the
availability calendar and the slot picker, the two surfaces the whole sketch direction is built around.

Output: a button contract, 13 solid focus recipes, 20 converted call sites, 9 deliberately unconverted
ones, and three green tests.
</objective>
