<objective>
Author the type scale, the three-step elevation scale, the four-step z-index scale and the motion budget,
and put the global reduced-motion reset and the solid focus colour into `@layer base`.

Purpose: Tailwind's default `shadow-*` compile to literal values, not `var()` references, so grove's
"pronounced elevation" is unreachable without named `@theme inline` steps — these are load-bearing, not a
naming nicety. And a co-located `font-semibold` (98 occurrences) beats a `--text-*` step's font-weight
fallback, so the weight, tracking and leading variables must themselves be per-theme or grove's headings
render identically to court's.

Output: an extended `@theme inline` namespace, per-theme type/elevation values in both blocks, a global
`:root` block for the tokens D-05 keeps constant, and three green tests.
</objective>
