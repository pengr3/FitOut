# Phase 13.1: Payment Reconciliation - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-08-22
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

**A booker who paid always ends up with a booking. A lost webhook becomes a delay, never a silent loss.**

This is backend and ops work — a scheduled reconciliation of money coming **in**, plus one user-facing
resolution path on the settling screen. It is NOT front-end polish and carries none of v1.1's REQ-IDs.

**In scope:** a reconciliation sweep over paid-but-unconfirmed bookings; a single live probe on the
pending surface after its poll cap; an operator alert when a webhook is found missing.
**Out of scope:** changing what counts as *authority* on a payment (see D-104); the host payout side
(`payout-reconcile.ts` already exists and is untouched); any UI beyond the settling screen's resolution.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

> **Namespace:** Phase 13 ran 13-CONTEXT D-60…D-103. This phase continues at **D-104**. The collision with
> PROJECT.md's own D-numbers is pre-existing and documented in `13-CONTEXT.md`; always qualify a citation
> as "13.1-CONTEXT D-104" or "PROJECT D-104".

### The authority rule — read before anything else

- **D-104: A server-side probe of PayMongo's API is AUTHORITATIVE. The browser never is.**
  PROJECT D-57 bans trusting `?paid=1` because a URL is spoofable — that ban is **unweakened** by this
  phase and must remain true at the end of it. But a server-to-server read of the provider's own record is
  the *same source of truth as the webhook, over a different transport*. Reconciling from it is legitimate
  exactly where trusting a redirect is not. **Never conflate the two**: no code path may confirm a booking
  because a query parameter was present.

### What reconciliation does when it finds a paid booking

- **D-105: Route through the SAME idempotent confirm path the webhook uses. Never a second one.**
  A replayed webhook and a reconciliation of the same payment must converge on one booking, one slot, one
  payout-ledger row, one email. Two roads to "confirmed" is how a customer gets charged once and paid out
  twice. If the existing path is not safely re-entrant, **making it so is part of this phase**.

- **D-106: The slot is the booker's if they paid inside their hold window.**
  PM's stated principle: *"payment is the final step so the slot must be final — you cannot pay on a slot
  that was already booked. We are the marketplace, we ensure things like that don't happen."*
  **The system already enforces this at the storage layer** and the planner must not re-implement it:
  `drizzle/0005_booking_exclusion.sql` is a GiST `EXCLUDE` over `(listing_id, unit, tstzrange(starts_at,
  ends_at))` **`WHERE status IN ('pending','confirmed')`** — so a live hold physically blocks any
  overlapping booking. The only gap is time: `HOLD_TTL_MINUTES = 15`
  (`src/lib/availability/units.ts:167`), after which an abandoned hold is swept so it cannot block a slot
  forever.
  **The rule:** compare PayMongo's `paid_at` against the hold's `expiresAt`. Paid **inside** the window →
  the slot is rightfully theirs, and any loss of it is FitOut's failure, not the booker's. Paid **after**
  expiry → they paid on a dead hold.
  ⚠ **This is why D-107's cadence is load-bearing, not a tuning knob.**

- **D-107: The sweep runs every 5 MINUTES, and that number is derived, not preferred.**
  A 5-minute sweep runs **three times inside a 15-minute hold**. A lost webhook is therefore reconciled
  *while the booker's own hold still occupies the slot* under the exclusion constraint above. That is what
  makes the PM's "this should never happen" nearly true in practice rather than merely aspirational.
  ⚠ **If anyone later lengthens this interval past the hold TTL, the guarantee silently dies.** Pin the
  relationship (sweep interval < HOLD_TTL) as an assertion, not a comment.

- **D-108: The gone-slot backstop is RETAINED, not deleted.**
  The PM's instinct is that paying on a taken slot should be impossible. D-106 + D-107 make it rare; they
  cannot make it unreachable (a genuinely late payment on a long-dead hold). Deleting the backstop would
  leave a paid booker with **no slot AND no refund**, which is worse than the case being fixed. Keep the
  existing D-58 `handleGoneSlot` behaviour — auto-refund on a refundable rail, and on QRPh/unrefundable
  the honest manual-return copy the PM approved (13-CONTEXT D-83) — and reach it through the same path.
  ⚠ `REFUNDABLE_RAILS` must not be widened. QRPh was re-probed 2026-08-21: identical `HTTP 400`.

### The user-facing half

- **D-109: The settling screen probes ONCE after its poll cap.**
  Someone actually watching gets an answer in seconds instead of waiting for the next sweep. The sweep
  remains the guarantee; this is only the fast path.
  ⚠ **Do NOT touch the poller's mechanics** — 13-07 froze them (ref-held router, interval-only setState,
  cleared on unmount, bounded attempts) and 13-20 preserved them with a zero-line diff.
  ⚠ **The copy must stay honest and CALM.** 13-20 (D-102) has just removed four claims from this surface;
  the heading is *"Confirming your payment"* and the money line *"We're waiting on your payment provider
  to confirm it."* A probe that resolves may state what it learned. A probe that learns nothing must not
  invent reassurance, and **STATE-05 still forbids any error affordance while the webhook is outstanding.**

### Visibility

- **D-110: A missed webhook is LOUD.** When reconciliation confirms a booking whose webhook never arrived,
  record it as an operator-visible event — the transport failing must be observable, not absorbed. Reuse
  the existing machinery (`recordAudit` + `needs_attention` + `src/inngest/functions/ops-alert-digest.ts`);
  do not invent a parallel alerting system.
  ⚠ `SUPPORT_EMAIL` is still `null` (`src/lib/site.ts:70`) and `tests/design/site-contacts.test.ts` is an
  inverted gate asserting zero support affordances in `src/`. **Operator alerting is server-side and must
  not introduce a booker-facing support affordance.**

### Scope guards

- **D-111: NO BACKFILL. New bookings only.**
  PM decision: *"only new ones, we are on dev, everything we do is experimental, and ensure we don't break
  on real-world transactions."* The sweep reconciles from ship-forward and must not reach back over
  historical rows. The two evidence rows above therefore stay `pending` — which is intended, and makes
  them clean fixtures.

- **D-112: Schema migrations are ALLOWED here if genuinely required — but justify one first.**
  v1.1's zero-migration rule (13-CONTEXT D-80 / GATE-06) is a **milestone polish** constraint; this is an
  inserted correctness phase, not polish. Even so, prefer the existing tables: `booking`,
  `paymongo_event`, and the `audit` table added at `0025` already carry what reconciliation needs.
  ⚠ **If a migration IS added, GATE-06's assertion and Phase 17's closing proof must be updated in the
  same change** — do not let a Phase-17 gate fail later on a decision made here.

</decisions>

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- The PM's framing, verbatim: *"we should be able to determine what the status of the webhook is so we can
  display the correct message… money was already deducted to our consumer, now we do not have it and I
  don't know if we would have the record that it's already paid for."* That last clause is the phase: a
  record that a payment happened must not depend on a single fragile delivery.
- Asked whether webhooks could simply be made lossless, the answer given was **no** — a webhook is an HTTP
  request over the public internet and fails on deploys, recycles and provider incidents. The guarantee
  comes from reconciling, not from hoping. Harden delivery *as well*, never *instead*.
- The governing sentence for any undecided copy on the settling screen: **describe FitOut's knowledge
  state, never the money's state.**

</specifics>

<deferred>
## Deferred Ideas

- **Confirming or refunding the two stuck rows** — explicitly out of scope (D-111). They are fixtures.
- **A server-verified `held` prop for the settling screen** — 13-20 found the client leaf cannot tell an
  exclusive hold (verified by the EXCLUDE predicate) from an open-capacity lease (which requires
  `expires_at > now()` and may have lapsed). Logged in Phase 13's `deferred-items.md`.
- **Hardening webhook delivery itself** (endpoint latency, retry visibility) — worth doing, but it reduces
  frequency rather than closing the hole. Reconciliation is the guarantee.

</deferred>
