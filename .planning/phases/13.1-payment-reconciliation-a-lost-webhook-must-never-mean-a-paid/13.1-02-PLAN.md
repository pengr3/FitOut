<objective>
Build the guarantee: a scheduled sweep that asks PayMongo what it already knows about every recent
paid-but-unconfirmed booking, and routes anything paid through plan 01's single confirm path.

`payout-reconcile.ts` reconciles money going OUT to hosts. Nothing reconciles money coming IN. This is
that function, and it is deliberately shaped as its mirror image — same cron/singleton form, same
`dbConn`-injectable step functions, same "the guard is the authority" discipline.

Purpose: a lost webhook becomes a delay measured in minutes, not a person who paid and got nothing.
Output: one registered 5-minute cron whose cadence is a proven inequality rather than a comment.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

|----------|-------------|
| Inngest → `/api/inngest` | Untrusted HTTP invokes scheduled work. `INNGEST_SIGNING_KEY` fail-closed in production is the existing authentication. |
| `payment-reconcile` → PayMongo REST | Outbound provider reads on a schedule; provider-controlled response fields enter the process. |

| `audit.meta` → ops digest email | Durable rows leave the system as email, an external service that forwards, archives and indexes. |
