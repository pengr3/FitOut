Configuration, registration, source controls, and historic evidence are prerequisites only; none is live-money proof.

## Hard-stop rules

1. Missing, stale, contradictory, redaction-invalid, unobserved, or unowned evidence immediately makes the affected row and broad availability **HOLD**.
2. An unexpected delivery, durable confirmation, recovery result, transfer state, refund/manual-return result, or reconciliation mismatch stops further related scope. Preserve the actual durable state, use the owned provider-side boundary, notify the accountable owners, and retain **HOLD**.
3. A browser return, source code, test result, webhook configuration, deployment configuration, function registration, provider inventory, or historic evidence is a prerequisite only. It cannot clear a live-money row.
4. An absent monitored acknowledgement, escalation route, response SLA, or observed alert acknowledgement blocks the related capability and broad availability.
5. An unavailable provider-side stop, cancellation, return, or escalation method blocks the related checkout or payout scope. Do not start an operation in order to discover the method.
6. A rail may become **VERIFIED** only with provider-verified refund capability and observed reconciliation, an owned manual-return record with observed reconciliation, or a concrete approved **OPTED OUT** record with named fallback and owner. Every other rail outcome is **HOLD**.
