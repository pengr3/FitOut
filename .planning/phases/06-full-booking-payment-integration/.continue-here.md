## Key research finding — PayMongo authorize/hold PER RAIL (load-bearing)
- **Cards:** HOLD-CAPABLE (`capture_type: manual` → `awaiting_capture` → `/capture` or `/cancel` void). **Sales-gated** "Advanced Card Feature"; hold **auto-voids after 7 days** (caps SLA ≤7d). Void almost certainly fee-free (docs silent).
- **GCash / Maya / GrabPay:** CAPTURE-ONLY. Refundable but **merchant still pays the fee on refunds** → ~2.5% bleed per decline. (Maya same-day = full-refund-only.)
- **QRPh:** CAPTURE-ONLY **and not API-refundable at all** → unusable for approval-gated bookings.
- Sources in HANDOFF.json (`research_finding_paymongo_per_rail`).
