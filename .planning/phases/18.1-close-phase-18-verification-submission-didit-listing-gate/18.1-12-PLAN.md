<objective>
Land **PM-C**: a host cannot create a listing until they are verified — server-side, with a refusal a
person can act on.

⚠ **The gate and the routing are DIFFERENT THINGS and this plan ships both.** The gate is
`createDraftListing`; it is non-bypassable and it is the requirement. The routing is what makes the
refusal **legible**, and without it this plan would ship a defect.

**FINDING F-2, measured:** `createDraftListing` has exactly ONE product caller,
`src/app/(host)/host/listings/new/page.tsx:21`, and it **swallows any failure into a silent
`redirect("/host/listings")` with no message at all.** A refusal added to the action today is a
silent bounce to the grid — the copy-about-a-check-that-never-ran defect one level up, and a direct
violation of Success Criterion 5.

⚠ **FINDING F-7:** `grandfathered` is **not** one of D-255's four refusing states, so a grandfathered
host **may** create listings. That is consistent with D-211/D-224 and needs no decision — but it must
be **written at the site and pinned by a test**, because a later reader will read "cannot create a
listing until they are verified" and try to fix it.

⚠ **Budget a whole task for fixtures.** Roughly twelve unit call sites and two e2e specs mint drafts
and will all fail at once.

Purpose: the gate the whole phase exists to make reachable, with a refusal that names the state, the
reason and the way out.
Output: one action gate, one routing read, one fixture sweep, one e2e spec.
</objective>
