## Decisions Made

- **The refund basis is now the charged total.** `quotedTotalCents`, not `spacePriceCents`. On the fixture booking that is ₱1,050 back and ₱0 retained.
- **Invert, don't delete.** The plan was explicit and it was the right call: the paragraph arguing that `false` made FitOut less generous to a defrauded booker than to one whose host merely flaked is exactly the argument the PM accepted, so it is now the flip's justification. A reader who deletes it loses the only record of why the line reads as it does.
- **Record the superseded answer.** D-209's verbatim `"booker 100% refund, but not the service fee / platform fee"` is still in `fees.ts` and `cancel-impact.ts`, explicitly marked superseded by D-236, so somebody who finds D-209 quoted in `18-PM-DECISIONS.md` does not conclude the live line is a bug and "fix" it back.
- **The three consequential test cases moved with the basis rather than being pinned to a literal.** Cases 8, 11 and 13 each got a one-line comment saying they read the basis as a consequence, so the next person to re-decide D-236 finds them instead of being surprised by them.
