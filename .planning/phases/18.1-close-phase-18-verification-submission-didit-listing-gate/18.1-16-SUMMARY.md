### ⚠ ONE HONEST LIMIT — the focus ring after a MOUSE press

Recorded rather than glossed, because the plan's criterion says focus must land *visibly*:

| Activation | `outlineStyle` | `outlineWidth` | `boxShadow` | Visible? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Keyboard** (`Enter` on the control) | `auto` | `1px` | `none` | **YES** |
| **Mouse** (click the control) | `none` | — | `none` | **NO** |

Chromium's `:focus-visible` heuristic does not match a *programmatic* focus whose most recent user
interaction was a pointer — a property `e2e/helpers/focus.ts:expectRing` documents in its own words
(*"a programmatic `.focus()` does not qualify in Chromium"*). **This is UNCHANGED from the anchor**: a
programmatically-focused anchor after a mouse click behaved identically, so it is not a regression
introduced by D-274, and it does not affect the screen-reader announcement, which is the thing the
mechanism exists for (a screen-reader operator reaches the control by keyboard). The keyboard path —
the one that matters for the announcement — **does** draw an indicator, and `expectRing`'s own
predicate would accept it (`outlineStyle !== "none" && width > 0`). Filed here rather than as a
deferred item because nothing is broken; if a future plan wants a sighted mouse operator to see the
move, that is a product decision about a ring, not a defect in this one.
