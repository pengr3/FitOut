<objective>
Land the vendor account and widen the port so an **asynchronous** provider can live behind it.

Research settled R1: Didit **can never** return a decision on the create call. Every verdict arrives
later, by webhook. So `VerificationProvider.verify` must be allowed to return a promise. The port's
own header already anticipated this and says the widening is *"one edit plus one `await`, not a
call-site sweep"* — this plan proves that census: there are exactly **two** non-test call sites.

This plan also decides **FINDING F-5** (how `VerificationDecision` copes with a *request* rather than
a *verdict*) and lands the additive `updatedAt` field the retry timestamp depends on.

⚠ **`port.ts`'s heading DELIBERATELY DOES NOT SPELL A CERTAIN DIRECTIVE.** `tests/ops/verification-port.test.ts`
case 13 reads its comment-stripped source and asserts that specifier appears **zero** times. Do not
"restore clarity" while editing it, and do not mention the Didit adapter's guard by name there.

Purpose: everything downstream — the adapter, the submission action, the webhook, the sweep — needs
the port to be async-capable and needs three credentials that only a human can mint.
Output: one human-configured vendor account, one widened contract, two awaits, one additive field.
</objective>
