<objective>
Close **deferred item D5**: an abandoned Didit flow locks a host out of their own verification for up
to **seven days**.

The PM found it by hand during 18.1-14's sandbox walk and ruled on 2026-09-02 that it closes **inside
this phase as a gap plan** rather than being carried forward. Their reasoning is this plan's north
star, and it is the only argument that matters here:

> the phase's stated goal is the verification path FitOut is legally required to have, and a path a
> distracted host cannot complete is arguably not that path.

**⚠ NOTHING IS BROKEN AND NOTHING BELOW IS A BUG FIX.** Three individually-correct behaviours compose
into a trap: the submission upsert's `WHERE` admits only `unverified` or a cooled-down `rejected`
(which is what stops a duplicate session and a second charge); the hosted-flow `url` is not stored
(its `session_token` is a bearer secret and D-263 argues against persisting evidence); and the
reconciliation sweep reads `"Not Started"` — a LIVE session — and correctly does nothing, because F-3
releases the row only on `Expired` / `Abandoned`. Meanwhile D-262/D-263's deliberate "no route to a
human" leaves such a host nobody to ask, and D-255 blocks listing creation. **Measured:** 128 minutes
after the press, `GET /v3/session/{vendor_ref}/decision/` returned `status: "Not Started"` with
`expires_at` exactly seven days out.

**The fix is proven, not proposed.** `POST /v3/session/` is idempotent over unfinished sessions on the
same `vendor_data` (ADDENDUM A3), confirmed first-hand against the operator's own stuck session:
the same `session_id` came back with a fresh, usable `url`. So re-pressing while `pending` hands the
host back their OWN flow — no duplicate session, no second charge, no new vendor state, and the stored
`vendor_ref` stays correct. **Do not re-litigate any of that; read `deferred-items.md § D5` in full
and build what it recommends.**

Purpose: a host who got distracted mid-signup — the common case, not the edge case — can finish.
Output: one new field on the ruling table, one adapter function, one widened `WHERE` with a
`created_at` CASE, one new copy label, one panel branch, and the cases that pin each of them.
