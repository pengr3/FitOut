<objective>
Close **D-231**: `title` and `description` join the material-field set.

A fake listing lies in its words as much as in its fields. LVER-03 shipped with the ROADMAP's stated
five (address, space type, capacity, photos, price) and `re-review.ts`'s own docblock records
title/description as **a deliberate exclusion and a recorded gap**. The PM closed the gap on
2026-09-01. This plan promotes them to seven.

⚠ **Three traps, all measured.** (1) The docblock at `re-review.ts:84-95` contains its own change
instruction — follow it literally. (2) `tests/listing/material-edit.test.ts` has TWO reds by design,
one of which says in its own comment *"If title/description are ever promoted into the material set,
UPDATE this rather than delete it."* (3) The fifth `listing_review_state` literal is **deliberately
absent** from `re-review.ts` and an acceptance grep counts occurrences of it — and there is **no live
Vitest test** enforcing that, which makes it more dangerous, not less.

Purpose: a host cannot rewrite what a space *is* in prose and stay approved on the strength of an old
review.
Output: two production edits, two updated test REDs, six new passing cases, two stale deferred
entries retired.
</objective>
