<objective>
Write the words before the surface that renders them, and give the email resend one owner.

`/host/verify` renders six states. The rule is `requests-signal.ts:56` — **a signal names the STATE,
the REASON and the WAY OUT** — and where there is genuinely no way out, says so plainly and stops.
That copy lives in a module, not typed into a page: *"A sentence typed into three page files is the
shape `requests-signal.ts`'s header describes by name."*

⚠ **Family 3 of the banned-language gate is the one that bites.** It forbids *"within N"*, *"N
hours/days/weeks"*, *"shortly"*, *"soon"* — a timeline nothing in the system agrees to keep. The
24-hour cooldown **is** kept by the system (it is literally the `updated_at < now() - interval '24
hours'` clause). The honest and gate-safe way to say it is the **instant**, not the **duration**:
an absolute, server-formatted timestamp. Never "in 24 hours". Never "tomorrow".

⚠ **The `pending` panel says there is no way out, and that is only honest because plan 18.1-06
mapped `Expired`/`Abandoned` to `unverified`.** If those had stayed `pending`, a host who closed the
tab would be reading "FitOut is waiting on the result" about a session that will never answer. That
dependency is why this plan sits after 18.1-06.

Purpose: every sentence the new surface will render exists, is scanned by the same gate as every
other host sentence, and has one owner.
Output: one copy module, one extracted helper, one rewired wizard, one extended corpus.
</objective>
