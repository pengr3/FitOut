### ⚠ ADDENDUM A7 IS SETTLED — and this is the section's most load-bearing reading

**`X-Signature` and `X-Signature-V2` carried the IDENTICAL 64-character digest on every delivery
observed.**

The vendor's two contradictory descriptions turn out to agree **in practice**, because Didit ships
already-canonical JSON: the raw bytes and the canonical re-serialisation of those bytes are the same
bytes, so the two HMACs are the same HMAC. **Raw-as-primary is therefore safe**, which is what the
shipped route does.

**The fixture in `tests/verification/didit-webhook.test.ts` was NOT replaced, and that is a decision
rather than an omission.** `tests/helpers/mocks.ts` computes `signDiditWebhook` over the raw body and
`signDiditWebhookV2` over `JSON.stringify(diditCanonical(JSON.parse(body)))` — two independently
settable headers, deliberately, so the fallback branch and the header-vs-body timestamp cases are
writable at all. Real deliveries make those two values COINCIDE; collapsing the helpers to one value
to "match reality" would delete the only instrument that can tell the two branches apart. The real
bytes agree with the shape the fixture already emits, so there was nothing to replace.

### THE REPLAY CHECK

**Retrieved 2026-09-02.** A **byte-identical repost** of the approve event, inside the freshness
window:

```
response      200
row           UNCHANGED
notifications NO second notification
audit         didit_verdict | denied | {"moved": false, "reason": "not_applicable"}
```

⚠ **THE IDEMPOTENCY HERE IS STRUCTURAL, NOT A LEDGER, AND THE DISTINCTION MATTERS TO ANYONE WHO
LATER TRIES TO "IMPROVE" IT.** There is no table of seen `event_id`s. The verdict `UPDATE`'s own
`WHERE` admits **`pending` only**, so a replay against any other state is a 0-row no-op by
construction — the same discipline as the submission path's guarded upsert. `event_id` **is** captured,
for traceability in the audit trail, and is **not** what makes the replay safe. A future reader who
removes the `pending` equality on the grounds that "the event id de-dupes it" would remove the actual
guard and leave the decoration.

`uat_listing_bookable` and two other published listings, so **its verification state decides
bookability and draft creation** for a large share of the suite. A UAT walk and an e2e run cannot
share a machine.

Baseline restored to `grandfathered` / `migration` before the run. **The failure set differs from run

the suite's denominator.
