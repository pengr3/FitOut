# Phase 18.1 Plan 10: The Verification Copy Module and the Resend's One Owner Summary

**Every sentence `/host/verify` will render now exists, is scanned by the same four families as every
other host sentence, and has exactly one owner** — six panels total over the enum so a seventh state
is a compile error rather than a blank page, a retry affordance that quotes the instant the database
lets the host through instead of a duration it would be promising, and the email resend extracted out
of the wizard the moment it acquired a second call site.

## What shipped

**1. `src/lib/host/verification-signal.ts` — the words, before the surface** (397 lines)

A total `Record<HostVerificationStatus, VerificationSignal>` with a `satisfies` clause, plus the
page's own three strings, the form's five lines, the author-named live region and D-264's composer.
The six rows are transcribed verbatim from `18.1-UI-SPEC § Surface 1 § The six states`, and each of
the spec's four notes is carried into the module as a comment at the row it justifies — including the
one that matters most: `pending` has no way out because FitOut stores `vendorRef` and not the
hosted-flow URL (`port.ts:76-98` has four fields and says not to add a fifth), so a "Continue the
check" link cannot be reconstructed and drawing one would be a control that acts on nothing.

**Totality is measured, not asserted.** Removing the `grandfathered` row reddens `tsc` with
`TS1360: Property 'grandfathered' is missing`, naming the state nobody decided about; restored, clean.

**Nothing with an owner is re-authored.** `suspended` re-states `SUSPENDED_HOST_SIGNAL`'s three
fields (the panel renders the shipped `HostingPausedNotice` component itself), and `rejected.reason`
is `HOST_REJECT_REASONS[0]` imported — the canned fallback D-265 already owns. The map is total
without the module becoming a second owner of anything.

**2. `src/lib/host/resend-verification.ts` — one owner for two sentences** (88 lines)

The wizard's local `resendVerification` body, verbatim: the `authClient.sendVerificationEmail` call
and both toast strings, now taking `{ email, callbackURL }`. The wizard's function is the call site;
its `@/lib/auth-client` import went with the body it served. Each sentence occurs exactly **once**
under `src/`, measured both ways. Client-safe: no guard, no DB import, no secret, and no directive
prologue — `src/lib/auth-client.ts`'s shape and its reason.

**3. `tests/listing/review-signal.test.ts` — the corpus reaches the verification surface**

`HOST_VISIBLE` goes from 9 strings to **49**: verification-signal's page copy, six panels and form
lines; the **composed** rejection sentence built through `composeDiditRejectReason` from a real
decline shape (so the rendered string is scanned, not a template); and the six sentences

## The two decisions that post-dated the plan

Both were taken by the PM on the day this plan ran, after it was written, and are recorded in
`18.1-RESEARCH § ADDENDUM B8`. Neither is mentioned in `18.1-10-PLAN.md`, and both land in its scope.

**D-273 — the hosted flow is mobile-only.** `is_desktop_allowed: false` is now a declared value. A
host who opens the check on a laptop reaches a flow that will not run there, and the PM's requirement
is that the instruction appears in the panel's **resting state, before the host presses anything** —
never as an error after a dead-end redirect. The research assigns the promise to 18.1-11, but 18.1-11
is the SURFACE: its own Task 1 says *"Do not type a sentence into this file"* and its acceptance
criteria grep it for prose literals. So the words are here, as
`VERIFICATION_MOBILE_ONLY_LINE`, with the WHERE and the WHY in its docblock; the rendering is
18.1-11's, and its plan already instructs it to import every sentence from this module. **What this
plan cannot prove is the WHEN** — that the panel renders it at rest rather than after a failure — and
that is recorded in the corpus test's own NOT COVERED list so the next reader under-trusts it.

**D-272 — the retry wording's trap.** The vendor cap is 7 per 7 days, but the per-module caps are
lower and deliberately unraised (2 document attempts, 3 liveness, 3 face match) and bound retries
*within one session*. A host can exhaust one sitting while holding almost all of their weekly
allowance, so a sentence about attempts being used up is false at the moment it is read. Two things
now keep it out: nothing in the module says it, and **case (17)** would notice if a later edit did.
`didit-verdict.ts` already holds the other half — an attempts-exhausted code is off the allow-list,
so the vendor's own wording never arrives either.
