<objective>
Implement **PM decision D-274** (`deferred-items.md § D9`): the `/ops` revealed host contact becomes
plain, copy-pasteable text. This is the ONLY thing holding phase 18.1 open.

**Purpose.** 18.1-14's hand-measure found the revealed email is a `mailto:` anchor that opens a mail
app. The PM decided on 2026-09-03 that the revealed contact details must not be clickable and must
not open a mail app — they are values an operator SELECTS AND COPIES, not links they follow.

**⚠ HALF THE PM'S SENTENCE IS ALREADY TRUE, AND THAT IS THE FIRST THING TO KNOW.** The PM said "the
email and number are clickable". Measured: the phone is *already* plain text
(`ops-contact-reveal.tsx:172`, `{contact.phone ?? PHONE_ABSENT}`), and the file's own header already
argues against ever linking it — a telephone-scheme href is *"meaningless on a desktop console and
would be a second unguarded scheme to argue about for nothing"* (D-271). **So this plan makes the
EMAIL match the PHONE. Do not re-litigate the phone; do not add a dial affordance; do not remove the
phone's plain-text rendering.**

**⚠ AND THE ANCHOR IS CARRYING SOMETHING THAT IS NOT STYLING.** The island has NO live region on the
success path, on purpose, and the reason is written at three sites (the component header, the
`ops-contact-refusal` row in `src/lib/design/live-regions.ts`, and `18.1-UI-SPEC § Surface 4`): the
anchor that appears TAKES FOCUS, and that focus move IS the announcement. Delete the anchor and a
screen-reader operator presses a button and hears **nothing at all**. This plan therefore removes a
link and KEEPS an announcement — see `<the_announcement_decision>` below, which is not a suggestion
but the shape to build.

**Output.** The row returns to strictly terminal, the 18.1-13 gate widenings are reverted (not left
dangling), OPS-06 closes in both representations, and the three Playwright readings 18.1-14 and
18.1-15 left OWED are discharged so the phase does not close carrying them.
</objective>

<the_announcement_decision>
## The replacement is DECIDED. Build this one; do not re-open it.

The anchor is the success announcement. Two replacements were weighed:

**(a) CHOSEN — the revealed value itself becomes the focus target.** Wrap `{contact.email}` in a
`<span ref={emailRef} tabIndex={-1}>` inside the same `<dd>` the anchor occupied. The effect keyed on
`contact` is unchanged; only the ref's element type changes.

**(b) REJECTED — announce through a live region.** Rejected for four reasons, all of them written
down before this plan existed:

1. `src/lib/design/live-regions.ts:1712-1718` does not merely omit a success region, it **forbids
   one**: *"⚠ SO NO SUCCESS REGION MAY BE ADDED HERE, and the reason is a mechanism rather than an
   economy."* That is a declaration inside a build-blocking design module, not a comment.
2. Adding a region re-opens a decision 18.1-13 took and argued at three sites, in a plan whose whole
   frame is *"a tightening, not a change of direction"*. A styling ruling is not a licence to
   re-decide the a11y mechanism underneath it.
3. It costs `LIVE_REGION_IDS` +1, a `LIVE_REGIONS` row, an `AUTHOR_NAMED_REGIONS` row, a second pin
   in `tests/design/live-regions.test.tsx`, and a rendered-region count in
   `tests/ops/ops-queue-row.test.tsx:545-553` that currently asserts the refusal region is the
   **only** `[role="status"]` on the row. Candidate (a) touches none of them.
4. GATE-03 rule 6 is *one region, one outcome*. A polite region beside a focus move is **two**
   announcements for one outcome — the exact thing rule 6 exists to prevent.

**Why (a) is not a downgrade.** Every property the anchor's focus move had is structural, not
anchor-specific: the target sits in the same `<dd>`, so a screen reader speaks it with its `<dt>`
context; its text content is the address, so the value is what gets spoken; and the caret lands on
the thing the operator came to read. What is LOST is the word "link" — which is precisely what D-274
asked to remove. `LIVE_REGION_FILES` stays at **31**, `DeclaredFileCountIsThirtyOne` does not move,
`LIVE_REGION_IDS` does not move, and `EXPECTED_OPS_ACTIONS` stays at **7**.

**Shape rules for the focus target — all four are load-bearing:**
- **`tabIndex={-1}`, never `0`.** A programmatically-focusable value that is not in the tab order.
  `booking-reference.tsx:145-149`'s reason, verbatim in intent. A `tabIndex={0}` would put a
  non-interactive text node into the tab order AND into `INTERACTIVE`, reddening assertion 2.
- **A `<span>` inside the `<dd>`, not a wrapper around both facts.** `<dl>`'s content model permits
  `dl > div > (dt+, dd+)` — a `div` nesting the two `Fact` divs is invalid and would put the
  label↔value association `row-card.tsx` protects at risk on a narrow screen. The target goes where
  the anchor was.
- **NO focus-outline suppression.** Do not add `outline-none` or any focus-visible override. The
  default ring is the sighted confirmation that focus moved and is the only visible trace of the
  announcement.
- **NO copy button, and no `user-select` CSS.** Plain text is already selectable. A copy control
  would be a FOURTH interactive descendant on the row and reddens assertion 2 — and the PM asked for
  plain text, not a new affordance.
</the_announcement_decision>
