The `assumption-delta` detector fired again (`pluralization`, on the same incidental word "second" in
the phase section). The incidental hit is not the interesting part; this round genuinely carries an
identity question, and it is exactly what both gaps are made of, so it is answered rather than waved off.

- **The noun that is now primary:** *the gate-bearing unit* — the thing an `if:` or a
  `continue-on-error:` can detach. Until now that noun was assumed singular and assumed to be **the
  job**. CR-02 introduced the second bearer (an individual step), and nobody re-asked whether the
  invariant still named the right noun. A second, parallel case: the **prefix source** was a *chosen*
  value (parameterized through `argv[2]`) where it should have been a *derived* one.
- **Decision: `promote`, on both.** (1) The general representation — *every unit of `gate-e2e` that can
  carry a condition* — becomes the primary subject of the unconditional invariant, and the job-level
  read is demoted to one element of that quantification rather than the whole check. Adding a second,
  step-only check alongside the still-job-only one would leave two partial answers to one question and
  reproduce exactly the asymmetry CR-02 is. (2) The prefix source is promoted back to fully derived —
  resolved from `import.meta.url` and from nothing else — and the configurable path is deleted rather
  than gated, because a configuration decision that can redirect a security control's input is a
  modeling decision wearing a flag's costume.
- **Invariant test accepted:** the widened `conditionalSteps` quantifier IS the contract test for (1) —
  it goes red the instant a future phase reintroduces the singular assumption by attaching a condition
  to a step. For (2), design-test cases 7 and 8 go red the instant an argument path is reintroduced.
</assumption_delta_decision>
