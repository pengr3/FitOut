<objective>
Introduce the booker-facing service fee end to end: compose it into the frozen quote at hold creation, snapshot the cancellation tier alongside it, disclose it as an itemised `Service fee` line at checkout, and show all-in rates everywhere the booker browses.

Purpose: D-74's fee funds the refund economics this phase creates, and D-75's invariant is that the number never goes up between browsing and paying. Two shipped comment blocks and one shipped string currently instruct the opposite (they encode D-50's superseded booker-facing half) — leaving them would cause a future reader to revert the fee line as a regression.
Output: fee-composed holds with a tier snapshot, a disclosed fee line, all-in browse pricing, and corrected shipped copy.
</objective>
