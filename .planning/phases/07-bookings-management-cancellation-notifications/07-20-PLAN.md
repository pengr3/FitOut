<objective>
Close the availability-discoverability gap and the cosmetic money/copy nits from Phase-7 UAT:

- **T4-hours (major, pre-existing Phase 3/4 gap surfaced in UAT)** — nothing links to the weekly-hours editor
  at `/host/listings/[id]/availability`; the wizard has no hours step and the host listings grid carries no
  link, so a published listing can sit at "No availability yet" and a booker sees an empty slot picker.
  **Chosen minimal fix + tradeoff (planner decision):** add a persistent "Availability" link from the Your
  listings card (the listing-management surface). This makes the editor reachable immediately and touches no
  gate. REJECTED alternative — making operating hours a hard publish-checklist requirement — is more forcing
  but (a) touches the load-bearing two-place publish gate (07-15: `publishSchema` + persisted re-read),
  (b) would strand already-published hours-less listings, and (c) needs a new "has hours" validation. That is
  a heavier product decision, deferred; the link solves the reported discoverability defect directly.
- **T7 (cosmetic)** — `formatMoney` drops the trailing zero (`₱307.5`, `₱300`) because
  `minimumFractionDigits` is 0; the ₱307.5 also leaks into the `refundLabel` notification payload.
- **T9 (cosmetic)** — the blocked-dates list on `/host/listings/[id]/availability` renders the raw enum
  `host_cancellation` instead of human copy.

Output: a discoverable availability link, money always at 2 decimals, and a human block label.
</objective>
