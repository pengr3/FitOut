<objective>
Repair the calendar-shape time bomb: `e2e/calendar-hit-area.spec.ts`'s shared `reachableCalendar` guard
hard-codes a six-row month grid, September 2026 renders five, and four of the fourteen CI failures are
that one assumption.

Purpose: this is the largest single root cause in the fourteen and the cheapest to close. It is also the
clearest demonstration that the red baseline is not "CI being CI" — this spec would have failed on a
laptop in any five-row month, and that is exactly the signal SC4 exists to restore.

Output: a helper that derives its geometry from the grid the page actually rendered, four passing tests,
and a triage transcript proving red before and green after.
</objective>
