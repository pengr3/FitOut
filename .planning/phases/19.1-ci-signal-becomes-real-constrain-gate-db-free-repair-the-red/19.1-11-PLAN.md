<objective>
Build the mechanism D-03 requires: a checked-in known-failures allowlist whose every entry carries a
written reason and which cannot silently grow — and close the one failure whose written reason is already
in its own source.

Purpose: an allowlist without a growth guard is a quarantine queue. The mechanism chosen here is entries
beside the tests they govern plus a pinned count in a build-blocking design test, because that test runs
inside the build, inside the job SC1 constrained, inside the required check SC5 installs — so the guard
inherits every protection this phase builds without adding machinery to the money-critical gate.

Output: `tests/design/e2e-known-failures.test.ts`, a resolved verdict for the fixed-advance-width spec, and
a census proved red in four directions.
</objective>
