reproduce *in the container* before choosing a mechanism, and if it annotates, the reason string must
**not** say "known open finding handed to Phase 17" — that sentence is false, and an allowlist entry

### Does the retry answer change plan 13's wall-clock budget?

**No — it confirms it.** An expected failure does **not** consume its configured retries: at
`--retries=2` the two annotated tests ran exactly once each, with no `(retry #N)` line, while only the
*unexpected pass* retried. So annotating `e2e/hold-countdown.spec.ts:443` turns three 3-minute timeouts
into one, and the **~6 minutes of the 44 that RESEARCH.md attributes to it are real**. Plan 13 may
assume the saving.

⚠ **The one cost that offsets it, and it is not in RESEARCH.md:** an *unexpected pass* **does** burn all
three attempts (and writes a trace on retry #1). A stale allowlist entry therefore costs three attempts,
not one — negligible on an 8ms test, expensive on one that times out.
