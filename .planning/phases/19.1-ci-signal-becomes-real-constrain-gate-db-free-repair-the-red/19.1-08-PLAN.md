<objective>
Settle the two failures whose root cause the CI job log cannot determine, and harden the helper whose
missing post-condition turns a lost click into a three-minute timeout.

appeared — roughly nine of the suite's forty-four minutes. `e2e/host-headings.spec.ts:964` shows a wizard
that would not advance past its location step with the save-state region reporting nothing at all.

Purpose: these are the two items RESEARCH.md leaves unresolved, and both carry spec messages that
explicitly forbid guessing. A wrong root cause here costs a plan; a trace costs one run.

Output: a traced diagnosis for each, a post-condition on the day-selection helper that converts a
three-minute timeout into a named one-second failure, and a recorded verdict for the wizard refusal.
</objective>
