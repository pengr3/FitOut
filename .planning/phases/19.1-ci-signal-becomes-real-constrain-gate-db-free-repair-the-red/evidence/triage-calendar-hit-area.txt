FINDING D-A2 — A SECOND DEFECT, PRODUCT-SIDE, MEASURED RATHER THAN INFERRED
================================================================================
19.1-03's own assumption block anticipated this branch: "If the repaired helper
leaves any of the four still red, that test has a SECOND defect and is triaged
separately rather than annotated." It does, and this is that triage.

WHAT IT IS. The six-row assumption Cause A found in the SPEC also lives in the
PRODUCT, in a second place the spec repair cannot reach:

  src/components/availability/availability-calendar.tsx - CalendarMonthSkeleton
    {Array.from({ length: 6 }).map((_, week) => ( ... ))}
  and its docblock arithmetic:
    "312   six week rows - 6 x (44 + 8) ... 409 against a resolved calendar
     measured at 409.19."

The plate therefore ALWAYS reserves six rows. The resolved grid renders five this
month. The plate is 410px, the grid it stands in for is 357.19px, and the delta is
52.81 — one week row, to the pixel (44 + 8 = 52, plus the 0.81 weekday-row
approximation the component's own docblock declares).

WHY IT IS REAL, NOT A TEST ARTEFACT. CalendarMonthSkeleton exists to occupy the
box the month grid will occupy — that is the whole of AC#15 / BFLOW-05, and the
component's docblock says so ("THE BOX IS THE ARGUMENT"). A plate 52.81px taller
than its twin is a 52.81px layout shift on resolve, on the listing detail page,
at every one of the three asserted widths, in both themes. The assertion is
correct; the product violates it.

HOW OFTEN IT IS LIVE — computed, not guessed (Sunday-start grid, 12 months from
2026-09):
  2026-09=5  2026-10=5  2026-11=5  2026-12=5  2027-01=6  2027-02=5
  2027-03=5  2027-04=5  2027-05=6  2027-06=5  2027-07=5  2027-08=5
  -> SIX-row months: 2 of 12.  FIVE-row months: 10 of 12.
The shift is live roughly TEN months in twelve. It went unseen because this spec
was authored on 18 August 2026 and August 2026 is one of the two six-row months —
the month the six was true in is the month the six was written in, in BOTH files.

WHY THIS PLAN DOES NOT FIX IT. It is a production change, outside 19.1-03's
declared files_modified, and the plan states "No production source file is
modified." It is also a PRODUCT FORK with two legitimate and materially different
repairs, which is an operator decision rather than an executor one:

  (i)  Make the PLATE derive its row count for the month it stands in for.
       Keeps the calendar's current appearance. Hazard: the plate is the
       PRE-HYDRATION paint, so the row count would be computed on the server —
       a server-vs-client month disagreement across a timezone boundary is a
       hydration mismatch, and this repository already treats timezone as a
       first-order pitfall.
  (ii) Make the GRID fixed at six rows (fixedWeeks on the DayPicker call site).
       One prop; removes the date dependence from the product rather than from
       the spec; makes the plate correct as written. Cost: every month then shows
       a trailing week of the next month, which is a visible UX change.

Both also ripple into e2e/skeleton-geometry.spec.ts, tests/design/
skeleton-a11y.test.tsx, src/lib/design/selector-contract.ts and the component's
own 409px docblock. Handed on as a finding; NOT repaired here, NOT annotated
here, and no tolerance widened to hide it.
