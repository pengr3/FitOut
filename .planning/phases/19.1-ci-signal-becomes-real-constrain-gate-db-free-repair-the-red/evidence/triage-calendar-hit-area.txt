DIAGNOSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. OBSERVED ROW COUNT = 5. The month grid rendered five week rows, because the
   month currently under test has five: `locator('[data-slot="calendar"] tbody tr')`
   "resolved to 5 elements" on 33 consecutive polls in every one of the four
   failures. September 2026 starts on a Tuesday and runs 30 days, so a
   Sunday-start grid needs five rows and not six.

2. ASSUMED ROW COUNT = 6. `WEEK_ROWS` is declared at :178 as a constant six —
   "Six, because a month grid always renders six week rows (`reachableCalendar`
   asserts it)" — and every height figure below the guard is computed from it.
   The guard at :261-265 is what pins the assumption; the spec's own failure
   message spells the arithmetic out: "The height figures below are 6 × (44 + 8)".

3. THE REPAIR DERIVES, IT DOES NOT RETUNE. `reachableCalendar` returns the
   observed row count read from that same locator, and each call site computes
   rows × (CELL_PX + WEEK_GAP_PX) = rows × (44 + 8) — the arithmetic the message
   already names, with `rows` supplied by the page instead of by the calendar the
   file was written in. NO TOLERANCE CHANGES: TOLERANCE_PX (1),
   SKELETON_TOLERANCE_PX (2), CELL_PX (44), WCAG_AA_TARGET_PX (24) and
   WEEK_GAP_PX (8) are all left at their HEAD values, and no assertion is deleted.
