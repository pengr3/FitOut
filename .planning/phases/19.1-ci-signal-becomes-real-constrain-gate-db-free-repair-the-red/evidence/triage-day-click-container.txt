VERDICT  THE CLICK IS RECEIVED AND REFUSED, AND `page.clock.install()` IS WHAT REFUSES IT. With
         Playwright's fake clock in force over `/listings/[id]`, the availability calendar's
         venue-timezone arithmetic lands ONE VENUE-DAY EARLY: the grid arrives with YESTERDAY (9/5)
         carrying `data-selected-single="true"` — a day that is `disabled` by
         `{ before: todayStart }` — and a click on the cell labelled "September 9th, 2026" marks
         9/8. Without the clock the same walk marks 9/6 on arrival and 9/9 after the click. The
         viewport is NOT the variable (C reproduces at 1280px); the clock is the only difference
         between B and A/C.

         THE FILE THAT OWNS IT IS `e2e/hold-countdown.spec.ts` — the repository's ONLY `page.clock`
         user among the twelve specs that reach this helper, and the only one of them that is red.
         It is an INSTRUMENT defect, not a product one: nobody using FitOut has a faked `Date`, and
         `deriveBookable` and every production source file are untouched by this plan.

FOUR THINGS THIS MEASUREMENT REFUTES, each of which was a live hypothesis when the plan was written:

  • NOT A LOST CLICK. `DETACHED=no`, above. The docblock's standing accusation is withdrawn at the
    site rather than left to mislead the next reader.
  • NOT 19.1-17'S STREAMING COMMIT WINDOW. `dayStagedHidden=0` on every read: no day button has a
    `[hidden]` ancestor, there is exactly ONE month grid in the document (`grids:1`), and the day
    locator resolves to exactly one node (`DAY-MATCHES=1`). The class the last two waves kept finding
    is measured ABSENT here — which is why this repair looks nothing like theirs.
  • NOT A HYDRATION RACE. All 33 day buttons carry `__reactFiber$` AND `__reactProps$` before the
    click (`hydratedDays:33, handlerDays:33`). The handler was attached; it ran; it computed the
    wrong day.
  • NOT A SHIFTED CLOCK. Page and Node agree to 2ms (`02:36:59.880Z` vs `.882Z`), the browser zone is
    UTC in both conditions, and both grids mark 9/6 as `Today`. Only the SELECTION moves.

The mechanism, named at the line: `availability-calendar.tsx:537` rebuilds the selection as
`new TZDate(day.year, day.month - 1, day.day, timezone)`, and `handleDaySelect` (:613-621) feeds it
back through the same constructor. That numeric-plus-zone constructor is what shifts under a replaced
global `Date`; one defect, both symptoms (the arrival state and the click result), which is why they
are off by exactly the same one day.

THE BUDGET DOES NOT MOVE, AND THE NUMBERS ARE WHY. Worst observed click-to-selected is 350ms against a
2,000ms budget — 5.7× headroom on a two-core image under load. The combined click-to-hours worst case
is 565ms, which brackets the laptop's 683ms rather than exceeding it, so the laptop figure was not
optimistic and nothing about this budget was resting on it. Widening the budget would ALSO not have
fixed the failure: with the clock installed the target day never becomes selected at all, so a longer
wait returns the same zero later. That is recorded because "widen the timeout" is the repair this
failure shape usually attracts, and here it is measurably useless.
