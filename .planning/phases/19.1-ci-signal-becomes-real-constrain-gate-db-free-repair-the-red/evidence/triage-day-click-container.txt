FOUR THINGS THIS MEASUREMENT REFUTES, each of which was a live hypothesis when the plan was written:

  • NOT A LOST CLICK. `DETACHED=no`, above. The docblock's standing accusation is withdrawn at the
    site rather than left to mislead the next reader.
  • NOT 19.1-17'S STREAMING COMMIT WINDOW. `dayStagedHidden=0` on every read: no day button has a
    `[hidden]` ancestor, there is exactly ONE month grid in the document (`grids:1`), and the day
    locator resolves to exactly one node (`DAY-MATCHES=1`). The class the last two waves kept finding
    is measured ABSENT here — which is why this repair looks nothing like theirs.
  • NOT A HYDRATION RACE. All 33 day buttons carry `__reactFiber$` AND `__reactProps$` before the
    click (`hydratedDays:33, handlerDays:33`). The handler was attached; it ran; it computed the
    wrong day.
  • NOT A SHIFTED CLOCK. Page and Node agree to 2ms (`02:36:59.880Z` vs `.882Z`), the browser zone is
    UTC in both conditions, and both grids mark 9/6 as `Today`. Only the SELECTION moves.

The mechanism, named at the line: `availability-calendar.tsx:537` rebuilds the selection as
`new TZDate(day.year, day.month - 1, day.day, timezone)`, and `handleDaySelect` (:613-621) feeds it
back through the same constructor. That numeric-plus-zone constructor is what shifts under a replaced
global `Date`; one defect, both symptoms (the arrival state and the click result), which is why they
are off by exactly the same one day.
