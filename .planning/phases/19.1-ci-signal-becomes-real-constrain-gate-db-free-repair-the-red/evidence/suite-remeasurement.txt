  WHAT IT IS. `src/app/(host)/host/layout.tsx:95` renders
  `<Suspense fallback={<SiteNav links={HOST_NAV_LINKS} />}><AmbientHostNav …/></Suspense>`. BOTH sides
  of that boundary mount a `NavDrawer` -> `ResponsiveDialog` -> Radix `DialogTrigger`, and a Radix
  trigger carries a GENERATED `aria-controls` id. The `+` in React's diff means the node exists only on
  the client, and the id it carries is React 19's CLIENT format (`_R_<hash>_`) rather than its SERVER
  format (`_r_<n>_`). The ids cannot agree, so hydration fails and the tree is regenerated on the
  client — on every `(host)` route, on this box, today.

  ⚠ AND THE LAYOUT ARGUES THE OPPOSITE, IN A COMMENT, AT THE SITE. `host/layout.tsx:90-93`:
      "the fallback is the identical `SiteNav` with no badges — which is byte-identical to the
       resolved zero-count markup, because D-65 hides the badge at zero."
  That argument is about the LINKS and the BADGE. It is true of both and false of the generated id,
  and nothing measured it until now. This is this phase's standing finding for the fifth time: a
  docblock asserts a property on the axis its author was thinking about, and is then read as a
  statement about the axis that actually matters.

  WHY IT IS THE MECHANISM BEHIND O11 AND O12. "This tree will be regenerated on the client" is a tree
  whose buttons are REPLACED. `e2e/helpers/booker-seed.ts:118-160` already records this exact shape one
