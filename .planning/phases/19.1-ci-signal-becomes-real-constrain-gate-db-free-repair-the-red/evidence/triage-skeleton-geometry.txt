3. IS THE DELTA DATE-DEPENDENT? NO — AND THE FILE'S OWN DOCBLOCK IS WRONG ABOUT THIS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STATED EXPLICITLY, BECAUSE THE PLAN REQUIRED AN EXPLICIT ANSWER EITHER WAY:

  THE OBSERVED DELTA IS **NOT** DATE-DEPENDENT. The residual Space column does not move with the
  calendar under the current fixture, on either rasteriser.

THE MECHANISM, MEASURED RATHER THAN ARGUED. `:909-925` states that "THE RESIDUAL MOVES WITH THE
CALENDAR, because the WHEN column is in it", and that the row height is therefore a function of the
date. That was true when it was written. It stopped being true when `260824-ght` added the SECOND,
long-titled listing — because that listing's booking is seeded at an ABSOLUTE venue-local day whose
composed label happens to be the WIDEST label this fixture can produce.

The When column is sized by the widest label among ALL rows on the list. The bookings list carries
five rows: three seeded at absolute instants (Nov, Jan, May) and two `requested` rows seeded RELATIVE
to the clock (dayOffset 5 and 6, `:1765-1785`) — only the last two vary with the date. Measured
inks, same run, same page:

    Thu, Sep 10, 9:00 AM – 11:00 AM   208.33   ← relative, dayOffset 5, moves with the clock
    Fri, Sep 11, 3:00 PM – 5:00 PM    195.20   ← relative, dayOffset 6, moves with the clock
    Mon, Jan 12, 9:00 AM – 11:00 AM   211.53   ← absolute
    Wed, May 20, 9:00 AM – 11:00 AM   219.03   ← absolute, and the widest thing on the page
    Thu, Nov 12, 9:00 AM – 11:00 AM   207.95   ← absolute, the row under test

Then the whole label space was swept in the When cell's own computed style — 7 weekdays × 12 months
× 31 days × the fixture's two seeded window spellings = 5,208 labels:

    Windows   max "Wed, May 20, 9:00 AM – 11:00 AM"  219.03      min "Fri, Jul 1, 3:00 PM – 5:00 PM"  185.34
    Linux     max "Mon, May 20, 9:00 AM – 11:00 AM"  223         min "Fri, Jul 1, 3:00 PM – 5:00 PM"  187

ON BOTH RASTERISERS THE MAXIMUM OF THE WHOLE SWEEP IS THE FIXTURE'S OWN ABSOLUTE MAY ROW. No date the
two relative rows can reach will ever widen the When column past a row that is already sitting at the
sweep's ceiling. The residual is therefore a CONSTANT — 183.70px on Windows, 166px on Linux — on
every date, and this failure is not the `[14-16]` / Cause A time-bomb class at all.

CONSEQUENCE FOR THE REPAIR: there is nothing to derive. Plan 03's calendar treatment does not apply
here; a static fixture value is the correct shape, and §7's CARRY line says so rather than leaving it
implied.

EVERY LINUX FIGURE IS A WHOLE NUMBER AND EVERY WINDOWS FIGURE IS FRACTIONAL. That is the mechanism in
one line: the container's Chromium quantises glyph advances to whole pixels, which makes every string

title that cleared its box by 22.53px on this laptop overflows it by 2px on the runner.

what makes "1" and "0" differ in width, which is `tabular-figures.spec.ts:361`'s 7px.

probe (l) shows the opposite cause producing 56.53px, so the height cannot tell them apart.

composed text, and `cbedf46` is intact and is still the last commit to touch the product file. The
