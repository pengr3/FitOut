19.1-11 — THE KNOWN-FAILURES ALLOWLIST: ITS ENTRIES, ITS GROWTH GUARD, AND THE PROOF BOTH WORK
==============================================================================================

WHY THIS FILE EXISTS
  D-03 requires a checked-in known-failures allowlist whose every entry carries a WRITTEN REASON and
  which CANNOT SILENTLY GROW. This file is the record: the verdict each entry rests on, the entry
  table and the derivation of the pinned count, the four mutations that prove the census red, and the
  chain from the census to the required status check.

  Nothing below prints a credential value, an environment-variable value or a signed URL. Only names,
  counts, measurements and outcomes.

    NOT EVIDENCE — `document.fonts.check()` returned `false` in BOTH environments, so it separates

── 1d. THE ROUTE TAKEN, AND WHY THE OTHER TWO WERE NOT ─────────────────────────────────────────────

annotate with the real reason.

  INTERCEPT — structurally unavailable. There is nothing to intercept: the woff2 is served by the app
    itself and was byte-identical across the two runs above. The difference is downstream of delivery.

because it is the more consequential half of this task. The message told its reader "the Google Fonts
