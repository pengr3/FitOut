# Phase 19.1 Plan 09: Settle and repair `skeleton-geometry.spec.ts:1807` Summary

**The host bookings row that "grew 20px" is fixture drift, not the reverted `260824-dbc` product regression — the Space cell wrapped and the When cell did not — and the drift is not the calendar but the CI container's whole-pixel glyph advances, which squeeze the residual Space column from 183.70px to 166px and make a 145.17px title measure 152px.**

## Accomplishments

- **Reproduced a failure that is green on this machine.** The prescribed local run passes: the row measures its declared 36.52px with 22.53px of clearance. Running the *same spec, same commit, same database* inside `mcr.microsoft.com/playwright:v1.60.0-noble` — the image `ci.yml:1561-1568` says `gate-e2e` runs in — produces CI's message and CI's received value to the last digit: `56.5px … Expected: <= 4  Received: 19.979999999999997`.
- **Named the cell, which is what the two candidate repairs turn on.** In the container the Space cell measured **2 lines** and the When cell **1**. The When cell structurally cannot wrap (`table.tsx:86` gives every `TableCell` `whitespace-nowrap`; the When cell carries no `whitespace-normal`), and `cbedf46` — the fix RESEARCH.md suspected of being reverted — is intact and still the last commit to touch the product file. Both dated candidates are exonerated.
- **Refuted the height as evidence.** 56.50px is within 0.03px of the file's own watched-red probe (l), which is the *opposite* cause. Reading the number and obeying the message would have sent a repair into a correct product file. The line counts are the evidence; they only exist because the reproduction was done in the container, where the serial describe's abort — the same abort that hid them from CI — was worked around with the file's own narrower grep.
- **Answered the date-dependence question with a sweep, and the answer is no.** Over all 5,208 labels the fixture can compose, the maximum on both rasterisers is the fixture's *own absolute May row*. The two clock-relative rows can never widen the When column past it, so the residual is a constant on every date. The file's docblock claim that "the residual moves with the calendar" was true when written and stopped being true when `260824-ght` added the long-titled listing; the comment is now corrected at the site.
- **Repaired the fixture in one non-comment line, and it closed two reds.** `HOST_LISTING_TITLE` 20 → 17 characters (`"Geo Courts Makati"`, the fixture's own venue city): 127px against a 150px content box in the container, 23px clear. The same run had already reddened `(agenda row · /host)` at 320px — 4 lines against a declared 3, same string, same source line `:1807` — and the same change closes it. **17/17 green in the container and 17/17 on Windows**, with four cases executing for the first time since `gate-e2e` began aborting the describe.
- **No tolerance and no declared shape constant moved.** `HOST_TOLERANCE_PX` (4), `TOLERANCE_PX` (2), `BOOKINGS_1280_ONE_LINE` (36.52), `BOOKINGS_1280_TWO_LINES` (57) and `HOST_AGENDA_ROW_HEIGHT` (112/72) are unchanged; `src/app/(host)/host/bookings/page.tsx` was not touched and its opposite-wrapping-rules comment survives with its warning sentence byte-identical.

## Decisions Made

- **The repair goes in the fixture, not the product.** The verdict was reached from two line counts and two cell widths measured on the gating rasteriser, plus the structural fact that the When cell cannot wrap. The height alone was ambiguous by 0.03px.
- **`"Geo Courts Makati"` rather than a shorter or a truncated string.** It is the widest candidate comfortably interior on *both* surfaces on the rasteriser that gates (23px clear in the Space cell; four characters above the agenda plateau's measured floor, two below its ceiling), and it is still a name a host in this fixture's city would give a space — which is the same argument the long title rests on.
- **Pinned, not derived.** Plan 03's calendar treatment does not apply: with no date-dependent band there is nothing to derive against. The `CARRY` line records the condition under which that stops being true (if the absolute May row is removed, the clock-relative rows would set the When column again).
- **The Windows figures are kept in the docblock rather than deleted.** They are not wrong; they are true of a machine that gates nothing, and the gap between the two columns is the finding a later reader needs.
