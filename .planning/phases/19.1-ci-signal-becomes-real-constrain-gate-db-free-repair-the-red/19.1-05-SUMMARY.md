## Decisions Made

- **The total is 55 (`baselines=11, ci=36, cross=8`), read from the checker's printed line.** All three spellings moved in Task 2's commit, with case 1 as the instrument that makes a silent divergence impossible.
- **Two allow-lists, not one.** `CHECKER_STEP_ALLOWED_KEYS` is `["name","run"]` and `BUILD_STEP_ALLOWED_KEYS` is `["env","name","run"]`. They differ by exactly one key and both declarations say why merging them would be a defeat rather than a tidy-up.
- **The two presence-not-value spellings both sit at JOB level** (cases 18 and 19) rather than one of them on the checker step as the plan's prose suggested. A softening key on the checker step also breaks that step's key-surface conjunct, so the red would arrive whether or not the predicate under test worked — the case would pass on somebody else's failure, which is 19-REVIEW.md IN-03's exact defect class. Case 21 then carries the same key onto the build step, where two invariants correctly fail and the case asserts the one it names.
- **Case 20 mutates a step INSERTED for the purpose**, so no allow-list covers it and the unconditional predicate is the only thing that can see it. That claim was checked rather than asserted: the same mutation on the tracked file fails **exactly 1 of 55**.
- **`withoutBuildStep` computes the step boundary from indentation.** A two-line deletion — the correct shape for the checker step — orphans the build step's `env:` map onto the preceding step, where YAML attaches it to the CHECKER step and the checker reddens on the wrong invariant. That was observed during evidence capture, not reasoned about.
