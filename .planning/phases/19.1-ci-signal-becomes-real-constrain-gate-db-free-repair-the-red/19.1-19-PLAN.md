<objective>
Make the availability calendar's today ring follow venue-local today — the decision the PM already took —
and turn the four visual references that expire at the next day rollover into references that do not.

Four baselines have been deliberately red since plan 19.1-12 un-minted them, because their content bakes in
the wall clock at render time: the ring marks the day the dispatch ran, and the host's member-since line
names the month the seed ran. Regenerating them without fixing that buys exactly one push. This plan fixes
the two causes, adds the assertion that stops the fix being dropped, proves the independence three ways, and
only then mints.

Purpose: the pixel-comparison job is red on every push alongside the functional suite, and a criterion that
says the run is not red is a property of the run. It is also the one job that will report without blocking,
which is precisely why a standing red on it trains everyone to ignore red.

Output: a ring that reads the same value its neighbours read, in both twin surfaces; a pinned fixture; a URL
parameter held by an assertion; a three-way clock-independence proof with a failing control; and four
regenerated references whose ring has been read out of the image rather than trusted.

⚠ Four tasks, one of them a blocking checkpoint, and the estimate is the phase's largest. **Task 2 is the
split point if execution runs long.** Tasks 1 and 2 are the whole fix — the source change, the fixture pin,
the URL parameter and the environment check — and they land without writing a single image; Tasks 3 and 4 are
the dispatch and the PM's answer, which are the costly, one-way-ish half. Stopping after Task 2 leaves the
four references red exactly as plan 19.1-12 left them, which is a coherent state and not a broken one.
</objective>
