## Decisions Made

- **The legal set is `{4, 5, 6}` and it is asserted as membership, not as a range.** A month of 28–31 days folded into seven-day rows can take no other shape, so a count outside the set is a broken grid rather than a different month. The message says so and tells the reader to repair the grid rather than widen the set.
- **The guard is two assertions, not one.** A separate `not.toHaveCount(0)` catches a zero-row grid explicitly, because `rows × 52 = 0` is a product every box would agree with — an empty pass wearing the shape of a measurement.
- **The two AC#15 failures were left RED.** Not annotated as expected failures (the plan prohibits it), not allowlisted (D-01 makes eligibility conditional on being unrepairable drift/flake — this is a repairable product defect awaiting a product decision), and emphatically not hidden by widening `SKELETON_TOLERANCE_PX`.

## Deviations from Plan

### Findings requiring escalation

**1. [Rule 4 — Architectural/product decision] FINDING D-A2: `CalendarMonthSkeleton` hard-codes six week rows**

- **Found during:** Task 2, immediately after the helper repair let the AC#15 tests past the guard for the first time in a five-row month.
- **Issue:** `src/components/availability/availability-calendar.tsx`'s `CalendarMonthSkeleton` renders `Array.from({ length: 6 })` week rows unconditionally, and its docblock derives its 409px height from `312 = 6 × (44 + 8)`. **The six-row assumption Cause A found in the spec also lives in the product.** Measured at all three widths in both themes: `skeleton {"width":288,"height":410}` vs `resolved {"width":288,"height":357.1875}` — Δheight **52.81**, Δwidth 0. That is exactly one week row (44 + 8) plus the 0.81px weekday-row approximation the component itself declares.
- **Why it is real:** the plate exists to occupy the box the grid will occupy — that is the whole of AC#15 / BFLOW-05, and the component's own docblock says "THE BOX IS THE ARGUMENT". So this is a genuine 52.81px layout shift on `/listings/[id]` when the calendar resolves. Computed over the next 12 months (Sunday-start): **only 2 of 12 are six-row months** (2027-01, 2027-05), so the shift is live **10 months in 12**. Both the spec and the component were written on 18 August 2026 — one of the two six-row months. The month the six was true in is the month the six was written in, in both files.
- **Why not fixed here:** it is a production change outside this plan's declared `files_modified` (the plan states "No production source file is modified"), **and** a product fork with two legitimate repairs that differ in visible UX — (i) derive the plate's row count, whose hazard is that the plate is the pre-hydration paint so a server-vs-client month disagreement across a timezone boundary is a hydration mismatch; or (ii) `fixedWeeks` on the DayPicker call site, one prop that removes the date dependence from the product but makes every month render a trailing week of the next. Both ripple into `e2e/skeleton-geometry.spec.ts`, `tests/design/skeleton-a11y.test.tsx`, `src/lib/design/selector-contract.ts` and the component's 409px docblock.
- **Handling:** triaged in `evidence/triage-calendar-hit-area.txt` §2 (FINDING D-A2) and the VERDICT; logged in `deferred-items.md`. **Not repaired, not annotated, not allowlisted, no tolerance widened.** This is exactly the branch the plan's own assumption block pre-authorised: *"If the repaired helper leaves any of the four still red, that test has a SECOND defect and is triaged separately rather than annotated."*
