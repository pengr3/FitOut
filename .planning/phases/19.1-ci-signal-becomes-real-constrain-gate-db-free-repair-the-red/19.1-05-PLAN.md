<objective>
Give the standing mutation suite its memory back and finish constraining `gate-db-free`.

Three things land together because they are one concern — the test file learns to read every checker-owned
constant from the checker and to throw when it cannot; SC3's dropped regression case is written in that
new idiom; and the three remaining SC1 invariants (the build step, the unconditional quantifier over the
job and every step, and the job-level execution-defaults block) are added as siblings of the `gate-e2e`
predicates rather than as widenings of them.

own load-bearing fix is reverted. And `gate-db-free` is still softenable three ways after plan 01 closed
the deletion and invocation vectors. Both are the same failure: a fix with no memory.

Output: a harness that throws instead of guessing, four to seven new standing cases, three new counted
invariants, and a watched-red pair for each.
