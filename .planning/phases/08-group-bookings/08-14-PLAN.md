<objective>
Close **WR-03** and **WR-04**, which are one finding wearing two hats: the phase uses two opposite conventions for whether the organizer is counted, and both surfaces that depend on the answer are wrong.

- **WR-03 (bears directly on SC4).** `createGroup` snapshots the listing's raw `max_occupancy` (`src/app/actions/group.ts:242`) and `claimSeat` admits that many **RSVP rows** — but the organizer is defined as an attendee who is *not* an RSVP row (`src/components/group/attendee-roster.tsx:33-35`, an explicit design comment). On a `maxOccupancy = 12` listing, 12 attendees say yes and the organizer makes **13 people in a space the host rated for 12**. SC4 says "hard-capped at the listing's capacity"; today it is capped one over.
- **WR-04.** `TopUpNudge` compares `confirmedYes` (organizer-EXCLUDED) against `declaredPax` (organizer-INCLUDED, per `src/components/booking/pax-stepper.tsx:125`'s own copy "This includes you"). With `declaredPax = 3` and 3 friends saying yes, four people are coming against a booking quoted for three — and `3 <= 3` keeps the nudge silent on exactly the first case it exists to catch.

## The convention, decided here, applied everywhere

Per **D-113** the organizer is attendee #1 and the base rate already covers them:

| Number | Basis | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `listing.maxOccupancy` | organizer-INCLUSIVE | it is the room's rating; `PaxStepper` already tells the organizer "up to {maxOccupancy}, this includes you" |
| `declaredPax` | organizer-INCLUSIVE | unchanged — it is what the booking was priced for |
| `booking_group.capacity_snapshot` | organizer-EXCLUSIVE | it caps RSVP ROWS, and the organizer structurally never occupies one → `GREATEST(max_occupancy - 1, 0)` |
| the organizer management surface | organizer-INCLUSIVE | its audience is the organizer, who is visibly row #1 of their own roster |
| the public invite page | organizer-EXCLUSIVE | its audience is an invitee claiming an invitee seat; **unchanged** |

**Existing `booking_group` rows are forward-only — no data migration.** D-111 makes `capacity_snapshot` a frozen creation-time snapshot precisely so a later change cannot retroactively move a cap people already answered against; rewriting shipped snapshots would violate the decision the column exists to encode. Groups created before this change keep their old cap. Every group created after it reserves the organizer's seat. This is stated as an explicit choice, not an omission.

**Incidental (noted, not a task):** the `createGroup` INSERT's own `WHERE` gains a positivity floor alongside the new expression, which also closes the write-side half of WR-09 for the group path. It adds no file and no task; WR-09 itself (the `draftSchema` half) stays out of scope.

Purpose: make SC4's "hard-capped at the listing's capacity" literally true, and make the D-114 signal fire when it should.
Output: one convention, applied in the snapshot, the meter, the roster's own documentation, and the nudge.
</objective>
