<objective>
Close the second half of **CR-02 (BLOCKER)** and `deferred-items.md` item 1 by wiring 08-12's column and client call into the two actions that create and re-price a checkout.

Today `src/app/actions/booking.ts:553` throws away `checkout.id`, so the session minted at ₱X survives forever alongside the session minted at ₱Y, and the confirm webhook — which keys purely on `reference_number` and confirms on `status='pending'` alone (D-57) — captures whichever lands first and silently captures the second too. The comment at `:536` still claims the key "makes a double-click / retry reuse the SAME session — never a second charge", which stopped being true the moment D-108 made the key amount-scoped.

**The ordering decision, stated up front because it is the load-bearing choice in this plan:** the expire happens **before** the re-freeze, and a failed expire **refuses the re-price**. The alternative (re-freeze first, expire best-effort) leaves the exact failure this finding is about — a booking quoted at ₱Y with a live session payable at ₱X, money captured, no refund, no alert. Refusing costs a booker one retry and loses nothing; proceeding costs real money with no recovery path. One guaranteed-correct amount beats two payable ones.

Purpose: make GROUP-01's "the organizer pays the full booking" mean exactly one booking-sized payment.
Output: a booking row that always names its live session, and a re-price that cannot create a second payable one.
</objective>
