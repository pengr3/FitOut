<objective>
Close the **production-fix half** of `deferred-items.md` item 5 — the live double-charge BLOCKER on the money path — and correct the three comments that assert a PayMongo guarantee the provider does not give.

Today `confirmBooking` (`src/app/actions/booking.ts:485`) reads the frozen amount, extends the hold, creates a hosted checkout session, and only AFTER the create writes `checkout.id` to `booking.checkout_session_id` (`:647`). It **never expires the previously-persisted session before creating the new one**. Because PayMongo does **not** honor the `Idempotency-Key` on `POST /v1/checkout_sessions` (probed live: two byte-identical POSTs mint two different, independently payable session ids), a plain double-submit of "Confirm & pay" mints a SECOND payable session and the booker is charged twice — ₱1,470 against a ₱735 booking in the 08-17 UAT, unrefundable because both were `qrph`. `updateDeclaredPax` (`:421-438`) already does exactly the right thing on a re-price; `confirmBooking` must mirror it. **This is wider than CR-02 and predates Phase 8: flat-priced bookings are equally exposed** — their `checkout:<bookingId>` key was believed to be a guard and is not.

**The load-bearing decision (stated up front):** the expire happens **before** the create, and a failed expire **refuses** to create a new session. The alternative (create first, expire best-effort) is the exact defect — a second payable session leaks and captures money that cannot be refunded on a `qrph` rail. Refusing costs a booker one retry and loses nothing; proceeding costs real money with no recovery path.

**Scope boundary — this plan ships the fix + a mock-backed DB regression + the comment corrections. The mandatory REAL-PayMongo-API proof lives in `08-19` (Wave 11), which depends on this plan.** Gap A is not fully closed until 08-19's non-mock test runs — a mock that echoes the idempotency key back is precisely what let this belief survive four plans of coverage.

Purpose: make GROUP-01's "the organizer pays the full booking" mean exactly one booking-sized payment on the SEQUENTIAL resubmission path (tab-switch / Back-then-reconfirm); a truly concurrent double-click stays an accepted residual (T-08-79), matching updateDeclaredPax.
Output: a `confirmBooking` that retires any prior session before minting a new one, fail-closed, and three comments that tell the truth.
