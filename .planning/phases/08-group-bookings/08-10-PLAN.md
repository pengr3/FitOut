<objective>
Close **CR-03 (BLOCKER)** — `declaredPax` reaches the price-freezing path completely unbounded.

`bookingCreateSchema.declaredPax` (`src/lib/validation/booking.ts:101`) has `.min(1)` and no `.max()`, and `createPendingHold` (`src/lib/availability/units.ts:438-450`) never selects `listing.maxOccupancy` and never clamps. A crafted POST therefore multiplies an attacker-chosen headcount straight into `quote.totalCents`, which becomes the frozen `spacePriceCents` (the payout gross basis), the `computeServiceFee` basis and the refundable basis — and a large enough value overflows the `integer` money columns into a Postgres `22003` that `mapBookingError` re-throws as a raw 500.

The contract the phase wrote for itself already says this is impossible: `src/components/booking/pax-stepper.tsx:20-21` states "the server clamps the value against the listing's own maxOccupancy on every call, so a crafted POST cannot declare 400 people." That clamp exists only on the OTHER entry point (`updateDeclaredPax`, `src/app/actions/booking.ts:376-377`). This plan puts it on the entry point that actually creates the booking, and bounds the shape at the schema so the int4 columns are unreachable.

Purpose: restore GROUP-01's "the organizer pays the full booking" as a server-authoritative statement on the per-head-priced path.
Output: a bounded `declaredPax` schema, an in-transaction listing clamp, and regression cases on a fixture that actually carries a `maxOccupancy`.
</objective>
