<objective>
Make a drop-in listing legible in search: the `Drop-in` badge on the type line, an all-in `₱/person` price,
no time range under any circumstances, the scarcity chip only when a date is in play, and a forward link
carrying the date alone.

Purpose: OC-12 and the search half of OPEN-04. The load-bearing rule is UI-SPEC **O2**: rendering
`Available 9:00 AM–11:00 AM on Fri, Aug 8` for a day pass would be a lie about what the booker is buying —
and the searcher's own `start`/`end` params make that the DEFAULT behaviour unless it is explicitly
suppressed.

Output: the search card branch, the host card price line, and a jsdom test file.
</objective>
