<objective>
Land the Phase-9 data model: the `open_capacity` occupancy mode, the dedicated per-head price column, the
`booking.open_capacity` denormalized flag, and — the easily-missed, load-bearing change — the **narrowing of
the `booking_no_overlap` GiST EXCLUDE to exclusive bookings only**. Apply all three migrations to the live
database and prove the new DDL behaves.

Purpose: without the EXCLUDE narrow, the SECOND open-capacity booking on a date is rejected as a
double-book (all open rows on a date share `unit = 1` and the identical `[dayOpen, dayClose)` window) —
a silent functional break the exclusive race test cannot catch. Without the migration applied, `tsc` and
`next build` stay green against a database that does not have the columns (the T-08-40 false-positive).

Output: schema.ts edits, three hand-authored migrations (55P04-safe split), an applied database, and the
four new global decisions recorded in PROJECT.md.
