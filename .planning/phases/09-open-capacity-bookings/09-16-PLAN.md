<task type="checkpoint:human-verify" gate="blocking">
  <name>Task 2: the drop-in walkthrough — confirm each step separately</name>
  <what-built>
Phase 9 shipped: the `open_capacity` occupancy mode with a per-person price and a people-per-day cap; a
date-pass booking flow with no hour picker; a DB-atomic admissions counter that hard-caps concurrent
bookings; spots-left in availability and search; drop-in-accurate time labels; and cancellation reuse with
the anti-resell auto-block deliberately skipped for drop-in bookings.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
**Confirm each step SEPARATELY — no blanket sign-off. Record the verbatim on-screen string for each, and
mark any unrun step NOT VERIFIED rather than assuming it (the 08-17 rule).**

1. **Host comprehension (OPEN-01).** Open `http://localhost:3000/host/listings/{id}/edit` and go to the
   occupancy step. Confirm: two equal-weight cards titled `Whole space` and `Drop-in passes`, neither
   coral; the drop-in body mentions a day pass, a price per person and how many people per day; the
   description line mentions you can change it later while you have no bookings still to come. Confirm the
   pricing step shows ONLY `Price per person` and `People per day` — no hourly rate, no day rate, no
   extra-guest pricing. Confirm the step counter reads **8** steps, not 9, and that the review step says
   `Drop-in passes are always instant — people book without waiting for your approval.`
   *Record: does the mode choice read clearly to you as a host, without any product knowledge?*

2. **The public page is day-shaped (OC-02).** As booker #1 open `http://localhost:3000/listings/{id}`.
   Confirm: a `Drop-in` badge next to `Availability`; the line `Pick a day — your pass is good any time
   they're open.`; **no hour chips anywhere**; picking the target date shows the date, a spots chip, the
   `Open …` hours line and `Your pass covers the whole day — come any time while they're open.`
   Confirm the rail shows `₱…/person`, `Service fee included`, `Up to 3 people a day`, and a pass stepper
   labelled `How many passes?` bounded at 3.

3. **Book and PAY for real (OPEN-02).** As booker #1 take **1 pass** → `Book this space` → the reserve page.
   Confirm the summary says a DAY and a pass, not an hour range, and that the breakdown reads
   `₱350.00/person × 1 pass`. Then `Confirm & pay` and complete the PayMongo test checkout.
   Confirm the booking reaches `confirmed` (webhook) at `http://localhost:3000/bookings` and that the row
   reads `… · Drop-in pass`, **not** a 06:00–22:00 range.
   *Record the exact `when` string shown on `/bookings` and on `/host/bookings`.*

4. **Spots decrement across bookers (OPEN-04).** As booker #2 (a different browser profile) reload the
   listing page and pick the same date. Confirm the chip now reads `Only 2 left` (cap 3 ⇒ threshold 1 …
   so at 2 remaining it may read `Spots available`; record what it actually says and at which remaining
   count the wording changes). Book 1 pass and pay. Reload as booker #1 and confirm the number moved again.

5. **Sell out (OPEN-03).** Take the last pass (either booker). Confirm the date now renders `Fully booked`,
   the day panel says `All 3 passes for this day are taken. Try another day.`, the date cell in the month
   grid is **not clickable**, and nothing is red. Confirm another date is still selectable.

6. **Partial grant (OC-07), if reachable.** With 1 spot left, try to book 3 passes. Confirm the reserve page
   shows the alert `Only 1 left for {date}` with BOTH money figures and the sentence
   `Nothing has been charged yet.`, plus a `Pick another date` button. *If the stepper's max prevented you
   from asking for 3, record that and mark this step NOT REACHABLE THROUGH THE UI (that is by design — the
   stepper is bounded by remaining — and the alert is reserved for a genuine race).*

7. **Search (OC-12).** Search with the target date AND a start/end time. Confirm the drop-in card shows the
   `Drop-in` badge, a `₱…/person` price and `Service fee included`, and that it shows **no time range at
   all**. Confirm the card disappears from results once the date is fully booked.

8. **Cancellation (OC-15/OC-16).** As booker #1 cancel the drop-in booking. Confirm the review page says
   `before the space opens` (not "before the session"), shows the refund breakdown and that the service fee
   is not refunded. After cancelling, confirm the date's spots-left went UP by one.
   Then, as the HOST, cancel the remaining drop-in booking from `http://localhost:3000/host/bookings`.
   Confirm the dialog lists **exactly TWO** consequences (refund + host fee) and **no** "Block … on this
   space" bullet. **Then confirm the date is still bookable for everyone else** — this is the Pitfall-5
   check and the single most important step in this walkthrough.

9. **Mode lock (OC-17).** With a future drop-in booking live, return to the wizard's occupancy step.
   Confirm the other card is disabled with a visible `Locked`, and that the alert names how many bookings
   are ahead, WHEN it unlocks (a real date and time with the city named), and offers `View your bookings`.
