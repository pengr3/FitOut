<objective>
Close **CR-01** (orchestrator-verified): `confirmBooking` refuses to start checkout once `starts_at` has
passed, and a drop-in pass's `starts_at` is the venue's **opening** instant — so a same-day pass can be held
but can never be paid for. Fork the D-94 cutoff on the persisted `booking.open_capacity`, exactly as
`createOpenCapacityHold` already forked its own expiry cap for the identical reason
(`src/lib/availability/units.ts:731-741`).

Purpose: this blocks the *ordinary* drop-in purchase — the headline walk-in use of a day pass. The claim
(`day_open_ok = dayClose > now()`) and the read model (`bookable = dayCloseUtc > now`) both advertise today
as buyable; only the payment step disagrees. The booker sees `HoldExpiredState` seconds after the hold was
minted, forever, and each retry silently occupies a spot for 15 minutes.

This plan also carries the **booking.ts half of NT-01**: after the fork, `"This session has already
started…"` is reachable only by an exclusive booking, which is the only booking it was ever true of. The
`cancel-booking.ts` half of NT-01 is 09-25's, together with WR-05.

Output: the forked cutoff, a new DB-backed integration gate that drives the REAL `confirmBooking` with a
same-day open row minted after opening, one recorded mutation, and an e2e case that removes the
`offset >= 3` blind spot from the browser proof.
</objective>
