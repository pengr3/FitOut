<objective>
Reuse the Phase-7 cancellation stack for drop-in bookings with exactly two changes: **skip the anti-resell
auto-block** on a host cancel (it would zero the whole date for every other booker), and reframe the
deadline copy from "before the session starts" to "before the space opens".

Purpose: OC-15/OC-16 and RESEARCH Pitfall 5. `cancelBookingAsHost` inserts an `availability_block` over the
freed `(unit, window)`. For a drop-in booking that window is the WHOLE DAY and the unit is the sentinel
`unit = 1` — so one host cancel would make the entire date read "Fully booked" for everyone who already
holds a pass. The refund, the host-cancel fee, the audit and the notifications all stay.

The seat release itself needs **no code**: `remaining` is a live SUM, so a cancelled row simply leaves the
occupying set.

Output: the host-cancel fork, three copy forks, and a cancellation test file.
</objective>
