<objective>
Instrument RESP-04's source half and add the two structural hooks its rendered half needs.

Purpose: RESP-04 says the named surfaces hold their structure "from **one** component tree". Two
different questions prove that, and neither alone is enough. The **source** question — does anything
render `{isMobile ? <A/> : <B/>}` — is an AST question and belongs in `tests/design/`, blocking inside
`npm run build`. The **rendered** question — is the identifying container present exactly once *in the
document* at 320/768/1280 — needs a real browser and lands in plan 17-09. That second question cannot
even be asked for search results or the calendar today: measured 2026-08-29, 49 test ids are declared
and neither of those two surfaces has an identifying container id. `skeleton-calendar` exists, but it
is the *fallback*, which is precisely why counting it answers the wrong question.

Output: one new blocking design gate, two new declared selector rows, two JSX attributes.
</objective>
