<objective>
Turn RESP-03 from *"seventeen named routes"* into *"every surface"*, and make the completeness itself
machine-checked.

Purpose: `src/app/**/page.tsx` is **29 files**; minus the two `src/app/dev/**` instruments that is
**27 production-reachable page routes**. Twenty are measured at 320px in both themes today. **Seven
have zero 320px measurement and are verified absent from `e2e/overflow-320.spec.ts` entirely** — all
seven behind the host or booker session gate, which is why they were never seed-free enough for the
AC#29 block.

settles what that means: the inventory is **enumerated, not remembered**. A surface silently absent
from the table is a surface that was never audited, and today nothing detects that absence. This plan
adds the assertion that does.

This is the second of three plans touching `e2e/overflow-320.spec.ts`. Plan 17-06 fixed the harness;
this one extends its coverage; plan 17-12 unskips the four error-boundary rows.

Output: seven seeded rows, one inventory assertion, and the no-wrap set's remaining reachable entries.
</objective>
