<objective>
Turn the audit's escalate-class discoveries into the phase's **second deliverable**, and re-prove the
closed inventories the sweep was most likely to have quietly opened.

Purpose: the one rule governing this phase is *an audit finding is a measurement first and a fix
second*. Where a fix would change a declared value, a layout decision, a piece of product copy or a
component's shape, **the finding is the deliverable**. Ten plans have now produced measurements; this
is where they become a list a PM can act on rather than ten SUMMARY paragraphs nobody re-reads.

17-CONTEXT **D-199** sets the cadence — batched, one list at phase end — and **D-200** sets the bar:
escalate-class items do not block this phase's completion, but **mechanical-class items are not
closeable-around**, so this plan is also the audit of the audit: every "may fix in place" item must be
verifiably fixed, and an unfixed one is an incomplete phase.

Output: one findings ledger, one recorded observation, refreshed blocked-row reasons, and a re-proof
of five closed inventories.
</objective>
