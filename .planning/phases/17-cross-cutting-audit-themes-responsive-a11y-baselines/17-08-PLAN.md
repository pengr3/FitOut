<objective>
Close GATE-02's keyboard gap: the five composite surface families that have no recorded walk.

Purpose: `e2e/auth-keyboard.spec.ts` walks **six auth documents, 59 stops**, measuring an indicator on
every stop. Those six are the only documents with a recorded walk in the whole suite. GATE-02 names
five families that have none — **the calendar, the slot picker, the wizard, dialogs and sheets** — and
it names them because each is a *composite* interaction where reachability is not implied by any
per-control assertion: a focusable `<div>` with a click handler is reachable and dead; a dialog that
traps focus is invisible to a forward-only walk; a dialog that dumps focus to `<body>` on close is a
silent dead end for a screen-reader user.

Two of the five properties have **no shipped assertion anywhere** — *escapable* and *returned* — so
this is the one plan in the phase that builds genuinely new measurement rather than extending an
existing one. The nearest shapes are named below; everything else is imported.

Output: one spec, five surface families, five properties each.
</objective>
